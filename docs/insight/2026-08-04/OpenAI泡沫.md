## Introduction: The Cult of Sam Altman

OpenAI 已经悄然成为近期经济史上最大的负债之一。没有它，现代 AI 产业就不复存在，数万亿美元资本支出 (capex) 的合理性也会瞬间化为乌有。
> OpenAI has quietly become one of the largest liabilities in recent economic history. Without it, the modern AI industry ceases to exist, and the justification for trillions of dollars in capital expenditure (capex) instantly evaporates. 

AI 泡沫并非源于实际的投资回报——无论是用收入、盈利能力还是切实可见的生产力提升来衡量。相反，它是感染了世界上最强大机构大脑的狂热精神错乱。它将一家单一的公司变成了历史上最大的资本错配。
> The AI bubble is not the result of an actual return on investment—whether measured in revenue, profitability, or tangible productivity gains. Rather, it is an episode of cult-like psychosis that infected the brains of the world's most powerful institutions. It transformed a singular company into the greatest capital misallocation in history. 

OpenAI 计划在 **2030 年底前烧掉超过 8520 亿美元**。它占据了微软、亚马逊和甲骨文 7480 亿美元的剩余履约义务 (RPO)，此外还有数十亿美元承诺给专门的基础设施提供商。它的失败将成为一个分水岭——AI 泡沫中的雷曼兄弟——最终将唤醒那些深陷集体精神错乱的人们。
> OpenAI intends to burn over **$852 billion by the end of 2030**. It accounts for $748 billion of the remaining performance obligations (RPOs) for Microsoft, Amazon, and Oracle, alongside billions more committed to specialized infrastructure providers. Its failure will be a watershed moment—the Lehman Brothers of the AI bubble—that will finally shake the afflicted out of their collective psychosis.

## The AI Bubble Is an OpenAI Bubble

OpenAI 是目前任何人关心 AI 的唯一原因。当 ChatGPT 在 2022 年 11 月发布时，恰逢科技行业江郎才尽、IPO 市场崩溃、加息终结了零利率时代，以及大规模过度招聘导致历史性裁员之际。
> OpenAI is the sole reason anyone currently cares about AI. When ChatGPT launched in November 2022, it arrived at the exact moment the tech industry had run out of ideas, the IPO market had collapsed, interest rate hikes had killed the zero-interest-rate era, and massive overhiring led to historic layoffs. 

大型科技公司极度需要一个借口来为其挥霍无度的支出和公然违规辩护。OpenAI 提供了这个理由：
> Big Tech desperately needed an excuse to justify its prodigal spending and flagrant rule-breaking. OpenAI provided that justification:

* **超大规模云服务商的转向：** 面临增长停滞的云服务巨头们利用 ChatGPT 快速获取用户的能力向投资者证明，*不*投资 AI 就意味着落后。
> * **The Hyperscaler Pivot:** Hyperscalers facing plateauing growth used ChatGPT’s rapid user acquisition to convince investors that *not* investing in AI meant being left behind.
* **Anthropic 的回声效应：** Anthropic 的存在主要是因为亚马逊和谷歌需要一种反制叙事来对抗微软对 OpenAI 的痴迷。
> * **The Anthropic Echo:** Anthropic exists primarily because Amazon and Google needed a counter-narrative to Microsoft's obsession with OpenAI.
* **基础设施单一化：** 除了 OpenAI 和 Anthropic 之外，对于当今正在建设的这种规模的计算力，几乎没有任何有机的市场需求。2025 年和 2026 年建造的每一个数据中心都是为了容纳下一个 OpenAI 或充当其房东而存在的。
> * **Infrastructure Monoculture:** Outside of OpenAI and Anthropic, there is virtually no organic market demand for compute at the scale being built today. Every data center built in 2025 and 2026 exists to house either the next OpenAI or to act as its landlord.

## Why the AI Bubble Can’t Survive Without OpenAI

你不能用风险投资来资助一个 AI 实验室。虽然 OpenAI 和 Anthropic 已经筹集了数千亿美元的股权融资，但它们真实的基础设施成本——GPU、TPU 和数据中心——完全是由超大规模云服务商补贴的。
> You cannot venture-fund an AI lab. While OpenAI and Anthropic have raised hundreds of billions in equity, their real infrastructure costs—GPUs, TPUs, and data centers—were entirely subsidized by hyperscalers. 

行业无法承受其主要主力租户的崩溃：
> The industry cannot survive the collapse of its primary anchor tenant:

1. **现金流之墙：** 超大规模云服务商已经无法仅仅依靠纯现金流来为其庞大的数据中心资本支出提供资金，这迫使他们严重依赖日益收紧的信贷市场。
> 1. **The Cash Flow Wall:** Hyperscalers can no longer fund their massive data center capex out of pure cash flow, forcing a heavy reliance on tightening credit markets.
2. **合同责任：** 不同于微软或亚马逊可能会为了保全面子而纵容 OpenAI，像甲骨文这样的供应商以及被死板协议锁定的硬件合作伙伴，在违约时会积极提起诉讼。
> 2. **Contractual Liabilities:** Unlike Microsoft or Amazon, which might indulge OpenAI to save face, vendors like Oracle and hardware partners locked into rigid agreements will aggressively litigate upon contractual default.
3. **用户基础悖论：** ChatGPT 数以亿计的周活跃用户是一个巨大的财务负担，只有极少部分人支付订阅费用，而广告收入也远远低于狂热的预测。
> 3. **The User Base Paradox:** ChatGPT's hundreds of millions of weekly active users are a massive financial drain, with only a tiny fraction paying for subscriptions and ad revenue falling woefully short of wild forecasts.

当 OpenAI 不可避免地耗尽资金时，它会连夜撤回其计算需求。由此产生的冲击波将冻结 AI 数据中心的债务，压垮像 NVIDIA 这样的芯片制造商，并摧毁每一个下游 AI 初创公司的立足前提。
> When OpenAI inevitably runs out of money, it will pull back its compute demands overnight. The resulting shockwave will freeze AI data center debt, crush chipmakers like NVIDIA, and destroy the foundational premise of every downstream AI startup.

## The Collateral Damage: Victims of the OpenAI Bubble

### 1. Consumers and the Memory Crisis
为了给 AI GPU 制造高带宽内存 (HBM)，主要内存制造商（三星、SK海力士和美光）将产能从传统的消费级 RAM 转移。在虚高的 AI 需求和大量（往往未兑现的）合作公告的推波助澜下，这种结构性转变引发了全球内存短缺。结果，笔记本电脑、智能手机和游戏机等日常消费电子产品的价格飙升。
> To build high-bandwidth memory (HBM) for AI GPUs, major memory manufacturers (Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron) diverted production away from conventional consumer RAM. Fueled by inflated AI demand and massive (often unfulfilled) partnership announcements, this structural shift triggered a global memory shortage. Consequently, the price of everyday consumer electronics—laptops, smartphones, and gaming consoles—has skyrocketed.

### 2. Retail Investors and the Enshittified Stock Market
OpenAI 深谙误导性公告的艺术。从与 NVIDIA、AMD 和博通之间虚幻的数千兆瓦数据中心协议，到悄然解散的电影级产品合作（比如 Sora 与迪士尼的合作），这些公关活动总是能人为制造出短期的股票暴涨。散户投资者在最高点买入，充当了机构的退出流动性，而商业记者则用不加批判地照本宣科取代了严谨的审查。
> OpenAI perfected the art of the misleading announcement. From phantom multi-gigawatt data center deals with NVIDIA, AMD, and Broadcom to cinematic product partnerships that quietly dissolved (like Sora's deal with Disney), these PR campaigns reliably engineered short-term stock spikes. Retail investors bought in at the top, acting as exit liquidity for institutions while business journalists traded rigorous scrutiny for uncritical transcript-reading.

### 3. SoftBank and Masayoshi Son
由于错过 OpenAI 早期阶段而产生的错失恐惧症 (FOMO) 的驱使，孙正义让软银承诺了数百亿美元的激进投资和过桥贷款。通过利用 ARM 的股票进行杠杆操作并抛售安全的持仓，软银将其资产负债表直接与 OpenAI 不确定的未来绑在了一起，这导致其信用降级，并让日本市场暴露在巨大的波动性之中。
> Driven by FOMO after missing out on OpenAI's early days, Masayoshi Son committed SoftBank to tens of billions of dollars in aggressive investments and bridge loans. By leveraging ARM stock and selling off safe holdings, SoftBank tied its balance sheet directly to OpenAI's uncertain future, earning credit downgrades and exposing Japanese markets to immense volatility.

### 4. Oracle and Larry Ellison
甲骨文斥资数千亿美元建设大规模的数据中心容量（“星门”项目），以服务与 OpenAI 签订的 3000 亿美元云合同。为了筹集资金，甲骨文背负了大量债务，其信用评级已被推至垃圾级的边缘。由于拉里·埃里森大量抵押其个人的甲骨文股票来支持数十亿美元的媒体竞标（例如派拉蒙-WBD 的合并），甲骨文股票的大幅下跌可能会引发其个人财务崩溃。
> Oracle has spent hundreds of billions building out massive data center capacity (the "Stargate" project) to service a $300 billion cloud contract with OpenAI. To fund this, Oracle has loaded up on debt, pushing its credit rating to the brink of junk status. Because Larry Ellison has heavily leveraged his personal Oracle shares to back multi-billion-dollar media bids (such as the Paramount-WBD merger), a sharp drop in Oracle's stock could trigger personal financial collapse.

## Conclusion: The Reckoning Awaits

AI 泡沫之所以能膨胀到史无前例的高度，是因为市场和媒体盲目地接受了未经证实的预测。通过将炒作视为基本经济指标，推动者们创造了一个环境，让公司即便从未证明过自己能够经营一家可持续、盈利的业务，也能达到万亿美元的估值。
> The AI bubble grew to unprecedented heights because the markets and media accepted unverified projections with blind faith. By treating hype as a fundamental economic metric, boosters created an environment where companies could achieve trillion-dollar valuations without ever proving they could operate a sustainable, profitable business.

当 OpenAI 泡沫最终破裂时，它将给全球经济留下一道永久的伤疤。科技公司不是你的朋友，风险投资家不是你的救世主，而山姆·奥特曼也不在乎你的死活。这个完全建立在虚无缥缈和借来资金之上、毫无方向且连年亏损的企业，由于科技行业的娇纵，最终招致的任何清算都是咎由自取。
> When the OpenAI Bubble finally bursts, it will leave a permanent scar on the global economy. Tech companies are not your friends, venture capitalists are not your saviors, and Sam Altman does not care if you live or die. The tech industry will deserve every ounce of the reckoning that follows its coddling of a directionless, loss-making enterprise built entirely on smoke, mirrors, and borrowed money.
