# 为什么所有人都在尝试制造固态电池？

### 文章背景与核心概要
随着全球电动汽车与储能领域的飞速发展，锂电池技术面临着能量密度上限与安全隐患的双重挑战。固态电池通过使用固态电解质替代易燃的液体电解质，有望大幅减少电池内部的辅助支撑材料、消除枝晶穿刺风险并提高安全性。目前，包括宁德时代、比亚迪、LG 和三星在内的全球电池巨头均在固态电池研发上投入重金。本文深入剖析了固态电池的基本物理化学原理、相较于传统锂电池的优势以及当前迈向商业化仍需克服的技术难题。

---

## 📋 概要

固态电池——即用固态材料替代液体电解质的锂离子电池——目前正获得来自宁德时代（CATL）、比亚迪（BYD）、LG 和三星等全球制造巨头的大规模投资。通过替换液体成分，这些下一代电池有望显著减少“材料脚手架”（辅助支撑结构），提升能量密度，并通过消除易燃液体和降低锂枝晶风险来增强安全性。尽管仍面临重大工程障碍（商业可行性可能还需要几年时间），但其变革性的潜力继续推动着全球数十亿美元的研发投入。

> Solid-state batteries—lithium-ion batteries that replace liquid electrolytes with solid materials—are currently receiving massive investments from global manufacturers like CATL, BYD, LG, and Samsung. By swapping out liquid components, these next-generation batteries promise significant reductions in "material scaffolding," improved energy density, and enhanced safety by eliminating flammable liquids and mitigating dendrite risks. While significant engineering hurdles remain (putting commercial viability likely years away), their transformative potential continues to drive billions of dollars in research and development worldwide.

---

## 1. 固态电池热潮

目前吸引全球巨大关注的一项电池技术是固态电池：这是一种用固态材料替代传统液体电解质的锂离子电池。

> A battery technology currently capturing immense global attention is the solid-state battery: a lithium-ion battery that replaces the traditional liquid electrolyte with a solid material.

* **行业应用：** 中国制造商[宁德时代（CATL）](https://cnevpost.com/2024/11/06/catl-solid-state-batteries-begin-sample-validation-report/)截至 2024 年拥有超过 1000 名专门从事固态电池研究的人员，同时[比亚迪（BYD）](https://electrek.co/2026/06/01/byd-all-solid-state-batteries-evs-by-2027/)、[LG](https://blog.lgchem.com/en/2026/03/25_solid_state_battery/)和[三星](https://www.samsungsdi.com/sdi-now/sdi-news/4782.html)等行业重磅玩家也在积极推进研发。
* **风险投资：** [截至 2025 年](https://www.electronicsweekly.com/news/business/solid-state-ev-battery-start-ups-in-sample-mode-2025-03/)，专注于固态电池的美欧初创企业已累计融资超过 40 亿美元。

> * **Industry Adoption:** Chinese manufacturer [CATL](https://cnevpost.com/2024/11/06/catl-solid-state-batteries-begin-sample-validation-report/) had over 1,000 personnel dedicated to solid-state research as of 2024, alongside active development by industry heavyweights like [BYD](https://electrek.co/2026/06/01/byd-all-solid-state-batteries-evs-by-2027/), [LG](https://blog.lgchem.com/en/2026/03/25_solid_state_battery/), and [Samsung](https://www.samsungsdi.com/sdi-now/sdi-news/4782.html).
> * **Venture Capital:** US and European startups focusing on solid-state batteries collectively raised over $4 billion [as of 2025](https://www.electronicsweekly.com/news/business/solid-state-ev-battery-start-ups-in-sample-mode-2025-03/).

相较于现有的液态电解质锂离子电池，固态电池展现出巨大的理论优势。替换液体电解质可以制造出更轻的电池，即单位输出能量所需的质量更小。此外，由于标准的液体电解质具有易燃性，用固态替代品取代它们能使电池更加安全，不易发生火灾。

> Solid-state batteries offer major theoretical advantages over current liquid-electrolyte lithium-ion cells. Replacing the liquid allows for lighter batteries that require less mass per unit of energy delivered. Furthermore, because standard liquid electrolytes are flammable, replacing them with solid alternatives could make batteries far safer and less prone to fires.

## 2. 电池基础：化学能如何产生电流

要理解固态电池为何具备这些优势，有助于在更广泛的化学能背景下审视传统锂离子电池的工作原理。

> To understand why solid-state batteries hold these advantages, it helps to examine how conventional lithium-ion batteries work within the broader context of chemical energy.

### 势阱的物理学原理

所有化学反应释放或吸收能量都是通过一个基本机制进行的：电子从一个势能阱移动到另一个势能阱。

> All chemical reactions release or absorb energy through a fundamental mechanism: electrons moving from one potential energy well to another.

* **重力类比：** 想象一个球停在山顶的浅槽中，山脚下有另一个浅槽。重力将球向下拉，根据其质量和高度赋予其势能。轻轻一推就会将球推离上方的浅槽，使其向山下滚动。这些势能转化为动能，最终转化为热能（摩擦力），直到它停在下方的浅槽中。
* **电磁现实：** 化学反应的作用机制类似，但用电磁力（带正电的原子核吸引带负电的电子）替代了重力。在放热反应中，给予一份初始能量（如加热）会将原子从其初始电子构型中“推离”，让它们“向山下滚动”进入更低能量的构型，并在此过程中释放热量或光子。

> * **The Gravity Analogy:** Imagine a ball resting in a shallow groove at the top of a hill, with another groove at the bottom. Gravity pulls the ball downward, giving it potential energy based on its mass and height. A small nudge knocks the ball out of its upper groove, sending it rolling downhill. That potential energy converts into kinetic energy and eventually thermal energy (friction) until it settles in the lower groove.
> * **The Electromagnetic Reality:** Chemical reactions operate similarly, but replace gravity with electromagnetism (positively charged nuclei pulling on negatively charged electrons). In an exothermic reaction, adding a kick of energy (such as heat) knocks atoms out of their initial electron configuration, letting them "roll downhill" into a lower-energy configuration and releasing heat or photons in the process.

### 锂离子电池如何利用这一点

锂离子电池通过锂元素来利用这些化学反应。当电池放电时，锂离子及其电子从负极（嵌在石墨层之间）“向山下掉落”至正极（嵌在磷酸铁锂 $\text{LiFePO}_4$ 等材料中）。

> Lithium-ion batteries utilize these chemical reactions via lithium. When a battery discharges, lithium ions and their electrons "fall downhill" from an anode (intercalated between sheets of graphite) to a cathode (intercalated in a material like lithium iron phosphate, $\text{LiFePO}_4$).

```
[ Anode (Graphite + Lithium) ] 
       │
       ├──> Ions pass through ──> [ Electrolyte ] ──> [ Cathode ]
       │
       └──> Electrons forced around via metallic conductor (Electrical Current!)
```

之所以青睐锂，是因为脱离锂的电子比脱离几乎任何其他金属的电子“下落得更远”。结合锂非常轻的相对原子质量（$\approx 7$），这些反应在单位质量上能产生极高的能量——粗略来看可与燃烧汽油相媲美。

> Lithium is favored because an electron leaving lithium has "farther to fall" than when leaving almost any other metal. Combined with lithium's very light atomic mass ($\approx 7$), these reactions yield an exceptionally high amount of energy per unit mass—roughly comparable to burning gasoline.

### 为什么电池的能量密度不如汽油？

尽管锂具有极高的能量潜力，但由于两个主要原因，锂离子电池在能量密度方面仍远远落后于汽油：

> Despite lithium's high energy potential, lithium-ion batteries lag far behind gasoline in energy density for two primary reasons:

1. **氧化剂问题：** 汽油发动机依靠周围空气中的氧气作为氧化剂，这意味着燃油车无需随车携带氧化剂。相反，锂离子电池必须在内部携带电子的终点（正极），这增加了大量质量。
2. **材料脚手架：** 构建锂反应以安全提取电流需要大量的附加结构质量。在负极，每个锂离子大约需要 6 个碳原子来构成石墨层。额外的质量还来自于电解质、隔膜和集流体。截至 2019 年，每 1 克参与反应的锂都需要约 70 克的支撑材料。

> 1. **The Oxidizer:** Gasoline engines rely on oxygen from the surrounding air to act as an oxidizer, meaning gasoline cars do not need to carry their own oxidizer. Lithium-ion batteries, conversely, must carry their electron destination (the cathode) internally, adding substantial mass.
> 2. **Material Scaffolding:** Structuring a lithium reaction to safely extract electric current requires extensive overhead mass. At the anode, every lithium ion requires roughly six carbon atoms for graphite sheets. Additional mass goes toward the electrolyte, separator, and current collectors. As of 2019, every gram of reacting lithium required about 70 grams of supporting material.

## 3. 固态电池的远景前景

固态电池的核心吸引力在于能大幅减少这种辅助支撑材料的重量。

> The primary appeal of solid-state batteries is a massive reduction in this supporting material scaffolding.

### 锂枝晶难题

在传统的液态电解质电池中，负极对锂离子的束缚较为松散。在充电过程中的某些特定条件下，锂离子可能会直接在负极表面获取电子，从而形成树枝状的金属结构，称为**锂枝晶（dendrites）**，而不是安全地嵌入石墨层内部。

> In conventional liquid-electrolyte batteries, the anode holds lithium ions loosely. Under certain conditions during charging, lithium ions can acquire an electron right at the anode's surface, forming tree-like metallic structures called **dendrites** instead of nestling safely inside the graphite sheets.

如果锂枝晶生长到足以刺穿正负极之间的隔膜，就会造成短路。这会引发剧烈且不可控的反应并产生巨大热量，往往导致灾难性的热失控和电池毁坏。

> If a dendrite grows large enough to pierce the separator between the anode and cathode, it creates a short circuit. This triggers a rapid, uncontrolled reaction that generates intense heat, frequently resulting in catastrophic thermal runaway and battery destruction.

### 固态解决方案

通过用坚固的固态材料替代液体电解质：

> By replacing the liquid electrolyte with a strong solid material:

* **抑制枝晶：** 坚固的固态电解质旨在从物理上阻挡锂枝晶的穿刺。
* **纯锂金属负极：** 消除枝晶威胁使得制造商能够彻底摒弃石墨嵌入结构，转向使用纯锂金属负极。
* **安全性提升：** 移除易燃的液体电解质大幅降低了火灾风险。

> * **Dendrite Suppression:** A robust solid electrolyte is designed to physically block dendrites from piercing through.
> * **Pure Lithium Anodes:** Eliminating the threat of dendrites allows manufacturers to dispense with the graphite intercalating structure altogether, shifting to a pure lithium metal anode. 
> * **Enhanced Safety:** Removing flammable liquid electrolytes drastically lowers the risk of fire.

### 当前前景与展望

固态电池尚未做好大规模商业化的准备。宁德时代董事长近期将其在技术成熟度等级中[评为 9 级中的第 4 级](https://electrek.co/2026/06/25/catl-solid-state-battery-level-4-2030/)，并指出商业可行性“尚待确立”。然而，固态电池最终将[更安全、能量密度更高且可能更便宜](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2425219121)的确定前景，确保了全球的投资与科研力量将继续勇往直前。

> Solid-state batteries are not quite ready for mass commercialization. CATL's chairman recently [ranked them at level 4 out of 9](https://electrek.co/2026/06/25/catl-solid-state-battery-level-4-2030/) on the technological readiness scale, noting that commercial viability "has yet to be established." However, the persistent promise that they will ultimately be [safer, more energy dense, and potentially cheaper](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2425219121) ensures that global investment and research will forge ahead.

## 📌 脚注与技术参考

1. **电子迁移机制：** 在放热放电过程中，电子之所以发生迁移，是因为电解质和正极提供了更低势能阱的理想位置。这会在电极之间产生净电荷不平衡。当通过导体连接时，电子流动以平衡电荷。由于到达的电子与到达的锂离子配对，持续的电流会一直流动，直到正极填满或负极耗尽。
2. **固态离子传输：** 在晶体固态电解质中，锂离子通过从晶格中的一个空位跳跃到下一个空位来进行迁移。热能导致离子每秒振动数万亿次；偶尔，某次振动拥有足够的能量和正确的轨迹，使其能够挤过周围的原子进入附近的空位。
3. **历史前车之鉴：** 20世纪80年代，一家名为 *Moli Energy* 的公司曾尝试推销采用锂金属负极的锂电池。然而，由于锂枝晶引发的短路导致电池起火，该公司最终被迫进行了[大规模召回](https://electricautonomy.ca/ev-supply-chain/batteries/2020-09-18/moli-energy-lithium-battery-technology/)。

> 1. **Electron Migration Mechanics:** Electrons migrate during discharge because the electrolyte and cathode offer more appealing locations with lower potential energy wells. This creates a net charge imbalance across the electrodes. When connected by a conductor, electrons flow to equalize the charge. Because arriving electrons pair with arriving lithium ions, continuous current flows until the cathode is full or the anode is depleted.
> 2. **Solid-State Ion Transport:** In a crystalline solid electrolyte, lithium ions migrate by hopping from one vacancy in the crystal lattice to the next. Thermal energy causes ions to vibrate trillions of times per second; occasionally, a vibration possesses enough energy and correct trajectory to squeeze past surrounding atoms into a nearby vacancy.
> 3. **Historical Precedent:** In the 1980s, a company called *Moli Energy* attempted to market lithium batteries featuring lithium metal anodes. They were ultimately forced into a [massive recall](https://electricautonomy.ca/ev-supply-chain/batteries/2020-09-18/moli-energy-lithium-battery-technology/) after dendrite-induced short circuits caused batteries to catch fire.
