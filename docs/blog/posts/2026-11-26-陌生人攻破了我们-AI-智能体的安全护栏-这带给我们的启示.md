# 陌生人攻破了我们 AI 智能体的安全护栏：这带给我们的启示

> # A Stranger Broke Our AI Agent’s Guardrails. Here Is What It Taught Us

### 文章背景与核心概要

在实际构建“让 AI 与企业私有数据对话”的应用时，许多开发者常误将提示词视作绝对可靠的安全边界。本文作者团队刚发布了一份关于如何限制 AI 智能体 (AI Agent) 权限的技术实战指南，短短数小时内便被一位素未谋面的外部开发者成功攻破，暴露了三大致命安全盲区：遗漏特定数据表的行级安全策略、允许智能体执行可篡改自身权限的动态查询，以及数据库连接池复用时的状态残留边界问题。这次被攻破的经历给所有开发者敲响了警钟——大模型绝不可能被纯文本指令彻底约束，真正的访问控制与权限防护必须下沉至底层数据库体系中，严格践行最小权限原则与自动化系统审查。

## 核心概要

> ## Summary

当一位开发者在某篇刚发布数小时的 AI 智能体安全实战指南中，轻而易举地绕过了其设定的安全护栏时，整个团队在 AI 数据访问设计上的严重盲区被彻底暴露在聚光灯下。本文深入剖析了这起攻破事件背后的三大关键安全漏洞——被遗忘的数据表权限、极具破坏性的智能体自修改查询能力，以及数据库连接池在状态复用时引发的边缘异常；同时阐明了为何诸如最小权限原则和严格的集成测试等传统软件工程基石，才是抵御 AI 越权利用与安全漏洞的唯一真正防线。

> When a developer successfully bypassed the guardrails of a newly published AI agent security walkthrough within hours of its release, it exposed critical vulnerabilities in how teams approach AI data access. This article breaks down three major security gaps—forgotten table permissions, dangerous self-modifying agent queries, and connection-pooling edge cases—and explains how traditional software engineering principles like least privilege and rigorous integration testing remain the only true defense against AI exploits.

---

## 核心要点

> ## Key Takeaways

* **提示词并非安全边界**：大模型很容易通过巧舌如簧的上下文绕过系统提示词；真正的访问控制必须牢牢扎根于底层数据库本身。
* **审视每一项赋予的能力**：赋予 AI 智能体的每一个工具或能力，都可能沦为攻击者反向渗透的潜在攻击面。
* **让安全审计全面自动化**：纯凭人脑记忆编写的测试，永远只能覆盖你恰好想到的角落。系统权限与数据表资产清单必须通过程序进行自动化审计。

> * **Prompts Are Not Boundaries:** Models can easily talk their way past system prompts; real access control must live inside the underlying database.
> * **Assess Every Capability:** Every tool or capability handed to an AI agent is a potential vector an attacker can exploit.
> * **Automate Security Audits:** Tests written purely from memory will only cover what you remember. System permissions and table inventories should be programmatically audited instead.

## 提示词绝非安全边界

> ## The Prompt Is Not the Boundary

几周前，我们针对几乎所有参与过的 AI 项目中都会碰到的一个棘手难题，发表了一篇技术实战指南：如何阻止 AI 智能体读取它不该碰的数据？文章给出的精简方案是——为该智能体在数据库中创建专有角色，仅授予其必要字段的读取权限，利用行级安全 (Row-Level Security, RLS) 将每条查询死死绑定在当前客户的作用域内，并编写试图刺探泄漏数据的测试用例。这是我们一直极力推荐的设计模式，而且在文章发布前，我们亲手跑通并验证了其中的每一行代码片段。

> A few weeks ago, we published a technical walkthrough on a problem we see in almost every AI project we take on: how to stop an AI agent from reading data it shouldn’t. The short version was that you give the agent its own database role, grant it only the columns it needs, pin every query to one customer with row-level security, and write a test that tries to leak data. It’s the pattern we recommend, and we tested every snippet before publishing.

然而就在文章发布的当天，一位素不相识的开发者在评论区留言了。他根据文章内容完整重现了整套架构，灌入了两名测试客户的数据，并运行了我们的测试套件。测试全部顺利通过。随后，他尝试亲手攻破这套防御体系，结果竟一口气找出了三种截然不同的方法，能以其中一个客户的身份登录，神不知鬼不觉地读取到另一位客户的私密数据。

> The same day, a developer we had never met left a comment. He had rebuilt the whole setup from the article, seeded it with two test customers, and run our tests. They passed. Then he tried to break it, and found three separate ways to read one customer’s data while logged in as another.

令人汗颜的是，他的这三处漏洞全部属实。我们在改动任何代码前一一复现了这些问题，迅速修正了文章中的纰漏，并在文章开头醒目地向他致谢。但相比于这几个具体缺陷本身，它们背后所折射出的深层现象才更值得警醒——那就是包括我们自己在内的大多数技术团队，目前在应对 AI 智能体安全时的思考盲区。

> He was right about all three. We reproduced each one before changing anything, fixed the article, and credited him at the top. But the bugs themselves matter less than what they say about how most teams are approaching AI agent security right now, ourselves included.

在各类“与数据对话 (chat with your data)”的功能设计中，最常见的致命误区就是把写给大模型的提示词当成真正的访问控制。一句诸如“只准回答关于当前客户的问题”的系统提示词，充其量只是一声恳切的“请求”。绝大多数情况下模型确实会乖乖照办，但只要遇上一句精心构造的巧妙提问、一份充满歧义的文档，抑或是一次恶意的[提示词注入 (Prompt Injection)](https://simonwillison.net/2025/Jun/16/the-lethal-trifecta/)，这道防线就会瞬间土崩瓦解。

> The most common mistake in "chat with your data" features is treating instructions to the model as access control. A system prompt that says "only answer questions about the current customer" is a request. Models follow it most of the time, and then a cleverly worded question, a confusing document or a [prompt injection](https://simonwillison.net/2025/Jun/16/the-lethal-trifecta/) gets past it.

OWASP 发布的顶级大语言模型 (LLM) 应用程序安全风险清单中，为这种底层缺陷确立了一个专属名称：[过度代理授权 (Excessive Agency)](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm062025-excessive-agency/)——意指 AI 系统被赋予了超出其工作职责所需的过多权限。OWASP 给出的建议，与安全工程师们几十年来反复强调的原则如出一辙：严格限制组件能够执行的操作，并将这些限制强制部署在组件无法通过“巧言令色”绕过的深层系统之中。这与[“AI 智能体需要的是明确的岗位权责规范，而不是更好的提示词”](https://www.unite.ai/ai-agent-governance-authority-limits/)背后的底层洞察完全一致：智能体的法定权限边界必须被定义在模型之外，而非写在模型内部。

> OWASP’s list of the top risks for LLM applications has a name for the underlying problem: [excessive agency](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm062025-excessive-agency/), meaning an AI system that has been given more permissions than its job requires. Their advice is the same advice security engineers have given for decades: limit what the component can do, and enforce the limit somewhere the component cannot talk its way past. It’s the same instinct behind the argument that [AI agents need job descriptions, not better prompts](https://www.unite.ai/ai-agent-governance-authority-limits/): an agent’s authority should be defined outside the model, not inside it.

对于需要执行数据库查询的智能体来说，这个“模型之外”的安全锚点理所当然就是数据库本身。我们当初的文章确实把限制设在了数据库层，但即便如此，我们依然在三处细节上栽了跟头。而这，恰恰是最值得大家深思与汲取教训的地方。

> For an agent that queries a database, that somewhere is the database itself. Our article put the limits there, and we still got it wrong in three places. That’s the part worth learning from.

## 漏洞一：那张被我们抛诸脑后的数据表

> ## Gap One: The Table We Forgot

我们为 AI 对应的数据库角色赋予了 `orders` (订单表) 和 `products` (产品表) 这两张表的读取权限。我们为 `orders` 表开启了[行级安全](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/ddl-rowsecurity.html)策略，但却自始至终忘记在 `products` 表上开启它。

> We granted the AI role read access to two tables, `orders` and `products`. We switched on [row-level security](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/ddl-rowsecurity.html) for `orders`. We never switched it on for `products`.

我们编写的每一个测试用例全都是围绕订单展开的，因为订单包含敏感交易，是我们当时唯一放心不下的数据。结果这位读者只是向智能体的“库存预警查询”工具提了个问题，就轻轻松松拿到了两位客户完整的专属产品目录。

> Every test we wrote was about orders, because orders were what we were worried about. The reader asked the agent’s "low stock" tool a question and got back both customers’ product catalogues.

这带来的教训绝不是轻飘飘的“下次记住所有表”。因为单凭人脑记忆，根本不可能做到滴水不漏。真正发人深省的启示在于：仅凭人脑记忆编写出来的测试，永远只能覆盖你当下记起来的东西。这位读者的修复方案比我们的高明得多：编写一段自动化测试，主动向数据库动态查询 AI 角色当前拥有读取权限的每一张表，一旦发现其中任何一张表缺乏行级安全策略，测试便直接报错失败。它完全不依赖任何人的记忆，而且在明年某位完全没读过这篇文章的新同事在数据库中新增一张表时，这项自动化保护依然固若金汤。

> The lesson isn’t "remember every table." People don’t remember every table. The lesson is that a test written from memory only covers what you remembered. His fix was better than ours: a test that asks the database for every table the AI role can read, and fails if any of them lacks a policy. It doesn’t depend on anyone’s memory, and it keeps working when a new table is added next year by someone who never read the article.

## 漏洞二：智能体竟能私自修改自身权限

> ## Gap Two: The Agent Could Change Its Own Permissions

在我们最初的设计中，业务程序通过在数据库中设置一个会话变量 (Session Value) 来声明当前请求归属于哪个客户，而行级安全策略则在底层将数据表的每一行记录与该变量进行比对。这个客户标识是由上层业务应用牢牢把控并注入的，模型本身绝无权限设置。

> Our design told the database which customer a request belonged to by setting a session value, and the security policy compared every row against that value. The application set it, never the model.

只要大模型仅能调用我们预先编写好的固定 SQL 模板，这套设计就是无懈可击的。但在原文中，我们顺带提了一笔未来可能的扩展构想——允许大模型自主编写并执行动态 SQL。这位敏锐的读者立刻向我们演示了这意味着什么灾难。在 Postgres 中，任何数据库角色都可以调用内置的[配置函数](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/functions-admin.html)来更改当前会话的运行时设置。这就意味着，一条构造巧妙的查询可以在执行中途强行篡改客户变量，从而肆意窥探其他客户的订单！更严重的是，同样的函数还能被用来直接关掉我们为了防止恶意慢查询拖垮数据库而设置的查询超时限制。

> That holds as long as the model can only call the fixed queries we wrote. We had also mentioned, as a later option, letting the model write its own SQL. The reader showed what that would mean. Postgres lets any role call a built-in [configuration function](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/functions-admin.html) that changes session settings. A single query could change the customer value halfway through and read another customer’s orders. The same function could switch off the timeout we had set to protect the database from runaway queries.

这种架构模式恰恰是当下一波智能体工具开发潮中最让我忧心忡忡的地方。我们赋予智能体的每一项自由权限，都可能成为攻击者借刀杀人的武器。[模型上下文协议 (Model Context Protocol, MCP) 的安全规范](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2025-11-25/basic/security_best_practices)在协议层面上也明确指出了这一点：工具必须被赋予其任务所需的最小权限。而放任大模型随意编写非受控的动态 SQL，与这一原则完全背道而驰。如今，我们向所有客户明确强调：默认必须采用预先命名的静态固定查询；而大模型自主生成的 SQL，必须通过严苛的白名单机制彻底审查其具体语句行为后，才被允许真正执行。

> This is the pattern that worries me most about the current wave of agent tooling. Every capability we give an agent is a capability an attacker can try to use through it. The [security guidance for the Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/specification/2025-11-25/basic/security_best_practices) makes the same point at the protocol level: tools should get the narrowest permissions their task needs. Letting a model write free-form SQL is the opposite of narrow. We now tell clients plainly that named, fixed queries are the default, and model-written SQL only runs after it passes a strict allowlist that checks what the statement actually does.

## 漏洞三：“故障安全”能否生效，竟然取决于你分到了哪个连接

> ## Gap Three: “Fails Closed” Depended on Which Connection You Got

在设计安全策略时，我们遵循了“故障安全闭合 (Fail-Closed)”的设计哲学。如果某次请求由于程序疏漏未声明客户归属，安全策略会默认拒绝返回任何数据，而不是将所有数据一股脑泄露出来。我们对此做过单独测试，当时表现完美。

> We had designed the policy to fail closed. If a request forgot to say which customer it belonged to, the policy returned nothing rather than everything. We tested it, and it worked.

然而，它之所以能正常工作，是因为当时测试运行在一个全新的空闲数据库连接上。但在真实的生产环境中，应用程序会通过连接池 (Connection Pool) 高频复用数据库连接；在一条已经处理过先前请求的旧连接上，那个原本应该“缺失”的变量并不会表现为空值，而是会被读取为一个空字符串。这时查询虽然不会泄露其他人的数据，却会直接发生崩溃报错，而不是优雅地返回空结果集。更诡异的是，系统是否会发生崩溃，完全取决于连接池当时随机将哪个底层连接分配给了当前的请求。

> It worked on a fresh database connection. In production, applications reuse connections through a pool, and on a connection that had already served a request, the "missing" value came back as an empty string rather than as nothing at all. The query didn’t leak data, but it crashed instead of returning an empty result, and whether it crashed depended on which connection the pool handed out.

单纯坐在电脑前肉眼审查静态代码，任何人都不可能发现这种隐蔽的边界缺陷。它只有在有人真正以贴近生产环境的运行方式实跑代码时才会暴露无遗。修复这一缺陷实际上只需要调用一个特定的底层重置函数；但要找出它，却必须依赖一位不带我们固有偏见、完全脱离我们先验假设的外部观察者。

> No one would have found this from reading the code. It only showed up because someone ran it the way it would really run. The fix was one function call. Finding it took an outsider who didn’t share our assumptions about how the code would be used.

## 我们在工程流程上做出的深刻改变

> ## What We Changed in How We Work

虽然文章中的代码已经彻底修正，但更有价值的财富其实沉淀在了我们的工程交付流程之中。

> The article is fixed, but the more useful changes are in our process.

1. **资产清单彻底胜过人脑记忆**：每当安全规则需要涵盖“每一张数据表”、“每一个工具”或“每一个 API 端点”时，我们现在一律通过向系统本身动态索取资产清单来进行自动化比对测试，彻底摒弃手工维护清单的陈旧做法。
2. **假设工具始终面临对抗利用**：智能体所拥有的每一项能力，我们都会预先假定它已被攻击者恶意利用。在为智能体赋予某个工具之前，我们会先反问自己：如果能与智能体对话的最阴险恶毒的黑客盯上了它，会用这个工具做些什么？如果答案包括能够篡改自身运行权限，那么这个工具就坚决不予上线。
3. **在高度拟真的生产环境下严苛测试**：我们要求所有安全护栏必须在贴近真实生产环境的条件下进行端到端压测：连接池复用、高并发竞争、失败重试机制以及真实规模的数据量。只能在开发者本地笔记本上跑通的安全护栏，根本谈不上是真正的护栏。
4. **全面拥抱并欢迎外部审查**：我们现在发自内心地渴望外部开发者的审视与刺探。正是因为将技术架构细节毫无保留地公开发表，才促成了这次及时的被攻破；而在公开发布仅一天之内，被一位友善的同仁在公开透明的环境下指出破绽，已经是一个技术团队所能期待的最幸运的结局。否则，这些隐患早晚会在某位客户真实的生产环境中演变成灾难性的安全事故。

> 1. **Inventory Beats Memory:** Wherever a security rule has to cover "every table", "every tool" or "every endpoint", we now test it by asking the system for its own list instead of writing the list by hand.
> 2. **Adversarial Tool Assumptions:** Every capability an agent has is assumed to be used against us. Before an agent gets a tool, we ask what the worst person who can talk to the agent could do with it. If the answer includes changing its own permissions, the tool doesn’t ship.
> 3. **Realistic Testing Conditions:** We test guardrails in the conditions they will actually run in: pooled connections, concurrent requests, retries, real data volumes. A guardrail that only holds on a developer’s laptop isn’t one.
> 4. **Embrace External Reviews:** We now actively want outsiders to look. Publishing the pattern in detail is what got it broken, and getting it broken in public, by someone friendly, within a day, is the best outcome we could have asked for. The alternative was finding these gaps in a client’s production system.

## 更深层次的行业反思

> ## The Broader Point

当前行业内围绕 AI 智能体的大量讨论，几乎完全聚焦于“能力”层面：哪个大模型推理更强、使用哪个框架编排更灵活、智能体接入了多少种花哨的工具。然而，鲜有人关注底层那套略显枯燥却至关重要的基石设施——即决定当大模型做出意料之外的行为时该如何兜底的系统权限、访问策略与自动化测试。

> Much of the conversation about AI agents is about capability: which model, which framework, how many tools. Far less is about the boring layer underneath, the permissions, policies and tests that decide what happens when the model does something nobody intended.

然而，正是这层枯燥的基础设施，维系着真正的安全防线；而且在本质上，它依然属于最传统的经典软件工程范畴。遵循最小权限原则、编写不依赖人脑记忆的自动化测试、引入非原作者视角的代码审查，几十年来一直是业界公认的最佳实践。AI 智能体的浪潮并没有颠覆这些常识；相反，它让忽视这些常识的代价变得前所未有的高昂——因为站在你权限防线对面的，不再是一个按部就班的传统软件模块，而是一个只要被下达指令，就会不知疲倦地尝试撞击每一扇敞开之门的自主系统。

> That layer is where the real risk sits, and it’s mostly ordinary software engineering. Least privilege, tests that don’t rely on memory, and review by people who didn’t write the code have been good practice for decades. AI agents haven’t changed that. They have made the cost of skipping it much higher, because the thing on the other side of your permissions is now a system that will try every door it’s given, as often as it’s asked.

我们当初的实战指南虽然在三处细节实现上存在缺陷，但其核心主张依然坚不可摧：AI 智能体的访问控制必须深植于智能体所触达的底层系统之中，绝不能指望写在提示词里。我们当初只是没能将这个理念贯彻得足够彻底而已。

> Our walkthrough was wrong in three places and right in its main claim. Access control for AI agents belongs in the systems the agent touches, not in the prompt. We just hadn’t taken that idea as far as it needed to go.

这也向当今每一位正在运行或构建 AI 智能体的工程师提出了一个发人深省的问题：如果今天有一位素未谋面的陌生高手花上一整天时间试图突破你智能体的权限体系，他们最先攻破的会是什么？

> Which raises a question worth asking of any agent you run today: if a stranger spent a day trying to break its permissions, what would they find first?
