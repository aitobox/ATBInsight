### 文章背景与核心概要
大型推理模型（LRMs）通过思维链（CoT）推理能够解决复杂任务，但这也常常带来冗长且重复的推理轨迹。虽然免训练的早期退出（Early-exit）方法尝试通过注入思考结束标记（`</think>`）来触发回答阶段从而缩短这些轨迹，但本研究表明，这种注入的标记并不能保证干净的阶段转换。相反，推理行为往往会延续到回答阶段——这种现象被称为**虚假思维链终止（spurious CoT termination）**。

作者假设，对注入的 `</think>` 标记分配的注意力不足是导致该问题的主要原因。为了验证这一假设，他们引入了**退出标记注意力偏置（Exit-token Attention Biasing, EAB）**方法。在多个模型和基准测试上的实验表明，增加对退出标记的注意力可以有效减少虚假终止现象，并缩短回答阶段的长度。这一发现揭示了通过外部匹配显式思考块格式来控制大型推理模型的局限性：单纯插入 EoT 标记符合了格式要求，但本身并不能保证实现预期的“推理到回答”的转换。

# `</think>` Doesn't Stop Reasoning: Analysis of Spurious CoT Termination

## Summary

> Chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning helps large reasoning models (LRMs) solve complex tasks, but often results in excessively long and redundant traces. While training-free early-exit methods attempt to shorten these traces by injecting an end-of-think token (`</think>`) to trigger the answering phase, this study reveals that the injected token fails to guarantee a clean transition. Instead, reasoning behaviors often persist into the answering phase—a phenomenon termed **spurious CoT termination**. 
>
> The authors hypothesize that insufficient attention paid to the injected `</think>` token drives this issue. To test this, they introduce **Exit-token Attention Biasing (EAB)**, demonstrating across multiple models and benchmarks that increasing attention to the exit token effectively reduces spurious termination and shortens answering-phase lengths.

## Abstract

> Chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning improves large reasoning models (LRMs) on complex tasks but often produces long, redundant traces. Recent training-free early-exit methods shorten these traces by choosing an intermediate point to stop reasoning. We study one such strategy that injects an end-of-think token (EoT, `</think>`) at this point to trigger the reasoning-to-answering transition, and find that the injected EoT does not always induce a clean answering phase. 
>
> Answering-phase generation can continue before the model regenerates another EoT, with the span preceding this regenerated EoT scaling with the reasoning tokens saved by early exit and exhibiting continued reasoning behavior. We call this *spurious CoT termination*, where reasoning-like generation continues into the answering phase. 
>
> We hypothesize that insufficient attention to the injected EoT contributes to spurious CoT termination and probe this hypothesis with Exit-token Attention Biasing (EAB). Across four LRMs, five benchmarks, and two early-exit methods, increasing attention to the injected EoT reduces spurious CoT termination and answering-phase length. These results reveal a limitation of controlling LRMs by externally matching their explicit think-block format. Inserting the EoT token conforms to this format but does not by itself guarantee the intended reasoning-to-answering transition.
