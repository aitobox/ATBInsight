# 约翰·特努斯应扭转苹果在广告泥潭中的滑坡

### 背景与摘要
这篇文章审视了苹果公司在隐私和广告策略上的转变。早在2014年，蒂姆·库克承诺“你不是产品”，建立了隐私保护的黄金标准。但近年来，苹果应用商店、Apple News+ 甚至是苹果地图中不断增加的广告，使其逐渐偏离了初衷。作者呼吁约翰·特努斯能够带领苹果回归2014年那般清晰的隐私优先立场，因为消费者信任的价值远超这些广告带来的边际收益。

## Summary

2014 年，蒂姆·库克有句名言：“在苹果，你不是产品。”这确立了用户隐私的黄金标准，它避开了大型科技公司以广告为导向的商业模式。十多年后，苹果滑向了一个滑坡：App Store 搜索由广告主导，Apple News+ 充斥着激进的互联网营销，并且广告也即将登陆 Apple Maps。虽然苹果的技术隐私架构仍然稳健，但普通消费者看到这些广告就会认为自己正在被跟踪。约翰·特努斯应该将苹果拉回到 2014 年以隐私优先的清明状态，重建远超这些广告所产生的边际收益的消费者信任。
> In 2014, Tim Cook famously declared that at Apple, "you’re not the product," establishing a gold standard for user privacy by steering clear of the ad-driven business models of Big Tech. Over a decade later, Apple has slid down a slippery slope: App Store search is dominated by ads, Apple News+ features aggressive internet marketing, and ads are coming to Apple Maps. While Apple's technical privacy architecture remains robust, the average consumer sees these ads and assumes they are being tracked. John Ternus should pivot Apple back to its 2014 privacy-first clarity, restoring consumer trust which far outweighs the marginal revenue generated by these advertisements.

## The 2014 Promise: "You’re Not the Product"

2014 年 9 月，在一系列备受瞩目的名人 iCloud 被黑客入侵事件之后，蒂姆·库克在 `apple.com/privacy` 上向客户发表了一封公开信（苹果已不再托管该 URL，尽管通过《华尔街日报》和互联网档案馆依然能找到备份）。
> In September 2014, following a series of high-profile celebrity iCloud hacks, Tim Cook published an open letter to customers at `apple.com/privacy` (a URL Apple no longer hosts, though copies persist via the *Wall Street Journal* and the Internet Archive). 

库克的信件有理有据、条理清晰，勾勒出了一个直截了当的商业模式：
> Cook’s letter was cogent, clear, and outlined a straightforward business model:

> 几年前，互联网服务的用户开始意识到，当一项在线服务是免费时，你就不再是客户，你就是产品。但在苹果，我们认为不应以牺牲您的隐私为代价来提供出色的客户体验。
>
> 我们的商业模式非常直接：我们销售出色的产品。我们不会根据您的电子邮件内容或网络浏览习惯建立个人档案，然后将其出售给广告商。我们不会对您存储在 iPhone 或 iCloud 中的信息进行“变现”……
>
> 我们业务中非常小的一部分确实服务于广告商，那就是 iAd。我们建立广告网络是因为一些应用程序开发人员依赖这种商业模式…… iAd 遵守适用于所有其他苹果产品的相同隐私政策……并且您始终可以完全选择退出。
>
> > A few years ago, users of Internet services began to realize that when an online service is free, you’re not the customer. You’re the product. But at Apple, we believe a great customer experience shouldn’t come at the expense of your privacy.
> >
> > Our business model is very straightforward: We sell great products. We don’t build a profile based on your email content or web browsing habits to sell to advertisers. We don’t “monetize” the information you store on your iPhone or in iCloud...
> >
> > One very small part of our business does serve advertisers, and that’s iAd. We built an advertising network because some app developers depend on that business model... iAd sticks to the same privacy policy that applies to every other Apple product... and you can always just opt out altogether.

## The Reality of 2026: Apple’s Ad Creep

虽然苹果隐私立场的某些核心要素依然成立，但那种“确信度”已经减退，库克声明的某些部分已公然过时。广告不再仅仅是苹果业务的“一小部分”：
> While core elements of Apple's privacy stance remain true, the "truthiness" has faded, and parts of Cook’s statement are flatly outdated. Advertising is no longer just "one small part" of Apple’s business:

* **App Store Search:** 搜索结果不仅包含付费广告（经常是关于赌场的），而且在视觉上被它们所主导，由于苹果增加了第二个赞助结果，情况变得更糟。
> * **App Store Search:** Search results not only feature paid ads (frequently for casinos), but are visually dominated by them, worsened by Apple's addition of a second sponsored result.
* **Apple News+:** 尽管在付费新闻方面提供了很高的价值，但该应用频繁展示奇怪的、质量低劣的由 AI 生成的网页广告。
> * **Apple News+:** Despite offering great value for paywalled journalism, the app frequently hosts bizarre, low-quality AI-generated web ads.
* **Apple Maps:** 免费使用，这意味着随着即将到来的广告整合，用户再次落入“你就是产品”（或者至少是“你的挫败感是产品”）的类别。
> * **Apple Maps:** Free to use, meaning users are once again falling into the "you are the product" (or at least, "your frustration is the product") category with upcoming ad integrations.

## Two Fronts Where Apple Lost Its Way

将这一轨迹与 Meta 在 Instagram 上默认的侵入性 AI 内容抓取政策进行比较，凸显出了鲜明的对比：苹果在隐私方面*确实*言行一致。然而，该公司在以下两个方面迷失了方向：
> Comparing this trajectory to Meta’s invasive default AI content-scraping policies on Instagram highlights a stark contrast: Apple *did* mean what it said about privacy. However, the company has gotten lost in the weeds on two fronts:

1. **压榨服务收入：** App Store 搜索中铺天盖地的广告，显然是为了创收服务，这背离了库克关于软件和服务纯粹旨在让设备变得更好的承诺。
> 1. **Squeezing Services Revenue:** The overwhelming volume of ads in App Store search clearly serves revenue generation over Cook’s promise that software and services are designed simply to make devices better.
2. **“私下的私密性”悖论：** 苹果对隐私有一种制度性的、几乎可以说是狂热的崇拜。即将出现在地图中的广告在技术上可能是“隐私优先的”，实现零个人跟踪或地理围栏保留。但公众相信什么呢？
> 2. **The "Privately Private" Paradox:** Apple has an institutional, almost religious fervor for privacy. Upcoming ads in Maps may technically be "privacy-first" with zero personal tracking or geofence retention. But what does the general public believe? 

对普通用户而言，“大型科技公司”就是一个整体。当人们在诸如地图等应用程序中看到广告时，他们会认为自己正在被跟踪，并且自己的位置信息被出售了。眼见为实，而广告本身就会侵蚀这种信任。
> To the average user, "Big Tech" is a monolith. When people see ads in an application like Maps, they assume they are being tracked and that their location is being sold. Seeing is believing, and advertising inherently erodes that trust.

## The Solution for John Ternus

蒂姆·库克 2014 年信件的威力不仅在于其清晰的语言——还在于苹果当时压根儿不展示广告这一事实。今天，苹果可以在技术上证明其广告尊重隐私，但公众永远不会完全相信。
> What gave Tim Cook’s 2014 letter its power wasn't just clear language—it was the fact that Apple simply didn’t show ads. Today, Apple can technically prove its ads respect privacy, but the general public will never fully believe it. 

此外，苹果每年通过与谷歌的 Safari 网络搜索流量获取成本合作赚取数百亿美元——无需自身展示任何广告，就能从广告生态系统中获取巨额利润。为了获取区区一小部分收入，新的 App Store 和地图广告反而玷污了苹果来之不易的声誉。
> Furthermore, Apple already rakes in tens of billions annually through its traffic acquisition cost partnership with Google for Safari web search—making a massive profit from the advertising ecosystem without ever needing to display an ad itself. The new App Store and Maps ads dirty Apple's hard-earned reputation for a fraction of that revenue.

约翰·特努斯有机会让苹果的隐私政策恢复到 2014 年时的那般清晰。在服务业务中扭转由广告驱动的膨胀，将为苹果赢得一种程度的消费者信任，这种信任远超为了粉饰财报而增加的营收。*“那些为你提供免费服务来向你展示广告的公司是在背叛你的隐私”*依然是一句极其有力的话语——特努斯应该为苹果重拾这句箴言。
> John Ternus has an opportunity to return Apple’s privacy policy to its 2014 clarity. Walking back the ad-driven bloat in Services would earn Apple a level of consumer trust that far outweighs quarterly revenue padding. *"The companies that offer you services free-of-charge to show you ads are betraying your privacy"* remains a remarkably powerful message—one that Ternus should reclaim for Apple.
