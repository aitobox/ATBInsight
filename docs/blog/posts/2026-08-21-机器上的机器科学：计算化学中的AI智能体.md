# 机器上的机器科学：计算化学中的AI智能体

> # Science Done on a Machine by a Machine: AI Agents in Computational Chemistry

## 摘要

这篇前瞻性文章探讨了计算化学中自主AI系统（agentic AI systems）的快速涌现与演变。从2024年的少数几个系统发展到2026年8月的近50个，这些AI智能体正从执行单一任务转变为全面自动化*in silico*（计算机模拟）实验——从实验设计、执行到分析与论文撰写。虽然目前人类监督仍然必不可少，但通用型智能体的快速崛起正威胁着将各类专用系统完全商品化。这一趋势促使科学界开始反思：在一个日益由机器驱动的学科中，既有的专家、教育工作者和学生未来将扮演什么样的角色。

> ## Summary
> 
> This perspective piece explores the rapid emergence and evolution of agentic AI systems in computational chemistry. Growing from just a handful of systems in 2024 to nearly 50 by August 2026, these AI agents are transitioning from performing isolated tasks to fully automating *in silico* experiments—from experiment design and execution to analysis and manuscript writing. While human oversight remains in the loop for now, the rapid rise of generalist agents threatens to commoditize specialized systems entirely. This trend leaves the scientific community questioning the future roles of established specialists, educators, and students alike in a discipline increasingly driven by machine-to-machine workflows.
