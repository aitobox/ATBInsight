# 毫无悬念，潜在篡位者 Fidji Simo 离开 OpenAI

**背景与摘要：**
本文报道了 OpenAI 二把手、前 AGI 部署 CEO Fidji Simo 因长期病假而辞去全职工作的消息。她的离职正值 OpenAI 战略重心转向企业级编程工具以应对 Anthropic 竞争之际，这也导致了由她主导的备受争议的“超级应用”项目被边缘化。业界此前就曾传出内部关于领导层继任问题的不和传闻，而她试图将传统社交媒体产品策略套用于生成式 AI 领域的做法也遭到了严厉批评。

> # Shocking No One, Fidji Simo, Would-Be Usurper, Is Out at OpenAI

## 📌 执行摘要

Fidji Simo 作为 OpenAI 的二把手以及前 AGI 部署首席执行官，在长期休病假后即将辞去她的全职职务。她的离去正逢 OpenAI 发生战略转向，公司正重点押注企业级编程工具以抗衡来自 Anthropic 的竞争，从而搁置了 Simo 备受争议的“超级应用”计划。行业观察家们早就注意到了公司内部的紧张局势，包括此前有关公司领导层继任问题的种种猜测。

> ## 📌 Executive Summary
> Fidji Simo, OpenAI’s No. 2 executive and former CEO of AGI Deployment, is stepping down from her full-time role following an extended medical leave. Her departure comes amid a strategic pivot by OpenAI toward enterprise coding tools to counter competition from Anthropic, sidelining Simo's controversial "superapp" initiatives. Industry observers have long noted internal tensions, including previous speculation regarding leadership succession at the company.

---

## 🚪 离职始末

根据 Berber Jin 和 Anissa Gardizy 在 [*华尔街日报*](https://www.wsj.com) 的报道：

> ## 🚪 The Departure
> According to a report by Berber Jin and Anissa Gardizy for [*The Wall Street Journal*](https://www.wsj.com):

> OpenAI 二把手 Fidji Simo 计划在长期病假结束后辞去全职职务。周四，她在发给员工的一份备忘录中传达了这一决定，表示自己的身体状况有所恶化，康复之路将比预期的漫长得多。她将转而担任公司的兼职顾问。 [...]
> 
> 在利润丰厚的企业级编程工具市场落后于 Anthropic 之后，公司突然将重心转向为企业构建由 AI 驱动的编程工具。Simo 曾领导了早期构建以编程为核心的“超级应用”的努力，OpenAI 已于今日发布了该应用，同时她还砍掉了一些诸如视频生成应用 Sora 等边缘项目。

> > Fidji Simo, OpenAI’s No. 2 executive, plans to step down from her full-time role after an extended medical leave. She communicated her decision in a note to staff Thursday, saying that her medical condition had worsened and her road to recovery would be much longer than anticipated. She will become a part-time adviser to the company. [...]
> > 
> > The company abruptly pivoted its focus to building AI-powered coding tools for businesses after falling behind Anthropic in that lucrative market. Simo led early efforts to create a coding-focused “superapp,” which OpenAI launched today, and cut side projects such as the video-generator app Sora.

## ♟️ 野心与内部动态

关于 Simo 的角色及其潜在的领导野心的猜测，此前曾在 [Ronan Farrow 和 Andrew Mantz 为《纽约客》撰写的关于 Sam Altman 的个人简介](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2026/04/13/sam-altman-may-control-our-future-can-he-be-trusted) 中被重点提及：

> ## ♟️ Ambitions and Internal Dynamics
> Speculation surrounding Simo's role and potential leadership ambitions was previously highlighted in a [Ronan Farrow and Andrew Mantz profile of Sam Altman in *The New Yorker*](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2026/04/13/sam-altman-may-control-our-future-can-he-be-trusted):

> 多位与 OpenAI 有关的高管一直对 Altman 的领导力持保留意见，并提议让曾任 Instacart 首席执行官、现任 OpenAI AGI 部署首席执行官的 Fidji Simo 作为继任者。一位了解最近讨论情况的知情人士告诉我们，Simo 本人曾私下表示，她认为 Altman 最终可能会下台。（Simo 对此予以否认。）

> > Several executives connected to OpenAI have expressed ongoing reservations about Altman’s leadership and floated Fidji Simo, who was formerly the C.E.O. of Instacart and now serves as OpenAI’s C.E.O. for AGI Deployment, as a successor. Simo herself has privately said that she believes Altman may eventually step down, a person briefed on a recent discussion told us. (Simo disputes this.)

## 📱 产品策略与市场现实

批评人士经常对将传统的社交媒体产品策略应用于生成式 AI 领域的做法提出质疑。正如一篇关于“超级应用”推出的评论所指出的：

> ## 📱 Product Strategy and Market Realities
> Critics have frequently questioned the application of traditional social media product strategies to the generative AI landscape. As noted in commentary regarding the "superapp" rollout:

> 上周泄露出来的这整个愚蠢的“超级应用”想法，听起来完全就像是一个负责运营 Facebook 应用的人会觉得是个好主意的东西。我预计的区别在于，Facebook 可以任由产品质量（和体验质量）被边缘化，因为他们的社交平台拥有如此强大的网络效应……而 ChatGPT 并没有这样的网络效应。Claude 在受欢迎程度上已经上升到与之几乎同等的地位，Gemini 也紧随其后……用户会直接选择切换到其他平台。

> > This whole dumb “superapp” idea that leaked last week sounds exactly like the sort of thing someone who ran the Facebook app would think is a good idea. The difference, I expect, is that Facebook is free to let product quality (and experience quality) fall by the wayside because their social platforms have such powerful network effects... There’s no network effect like that for ChatGPT. Claude is already rising to near-equal status in popularity, and Gemini isn’t far behind... People will just switch.
