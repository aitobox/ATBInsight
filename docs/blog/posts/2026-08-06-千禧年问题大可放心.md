## 前瞻预告（2000 年）

> ## Preview of Coming Attractions (The Year 2000)

* **Maxis 与《模拟人生》（*The Sims*）：** 我们现在已经深陷这一主题中，因此我认为无需多做解释。
* **赛车模拟游戏（Driving Sims）：** 这里一个被严重忽视的领域。虽然我无法完全补救这一缺失，但我可以探讨这类生活方式导向的游戏是如何诞生的，并聊聊我唯一真正沉迷过的一款赛车游戏（提示：与保时捷有关）。
* **《百战天虫/旅鼠历险记》（*Lemmings*）系列：** 该系列在后期的反响略显平淡，但至少以最后一款真正出色的作品画上了句号。
* **重磅 RPG 游戏：** 《Deus Ex》（杀出重围）、《Diablo II》（暗黑破坏神 II）、《Icewind Dale》（冰风谷）以及《Baldur’s Gate II》（博德之门 II）。
* **俄罗斯 CRPG：** 俄罗斯人来了！最突出的作品是《Evil Islands》（恶魔岛），一款独具特色且引人入胜的 CRPG。
* **城市建造类游戏：** Impressions Games 及其历史题材城市建造游戏。
* **Her Interactive：** 多年来我一直承诺要写写那群通过看似不可能的载体——互动式《Nancy Drew》（南茜·朱尔）——收获名利的团队。今年我终于要兑现这个承诺了。不，我是说真的！
* **冒险游戏类型：** 持续寻找存在感的挣扎：《Discworld Noir》（碟形世界：黑幕）；《In Cold Blood》（冷酷无情）；《Stupid Invaders》（傻瓜入侵者）；《Escape from Monkey Island》（逃离猴岛）；《RealMyst》（真实神秘岛）；以及《Fairy Tale About Father Frost, Ivan, and Nastya》（尝试把这最后一个名字快速念三遍...）。虽然这份清单谈不上振奋人心，但势在必行。
* ***《最长的旅程》（The Longest Journey）*：** 鉴于其备受好评，这款冒险游戏绝对值得拥有一个独立的项目列表。
* ***《无人永生》（No One Lives Forever）*：** 为日益黑暗、睾酮过剩的 FPS 游戏类型注入了一股亟需的时髦、轻快与奇思妙想的新鲜空气（哪怕它完全搬套了《奥斯汀·鲍尔斯》的审美）。
* **欧洲策略游戏（*Aufbaustrategie*）：** 诸如《Settlers》（工人物语）系列特别是《Anno 1602》（创世纪 1602）在德国和欧洲超乎寻常的高人气，后者在世纪之交成为了德国有史以来最畅销的游戏。
* ***《Technomage: Return of Eternity》*：** 我在研究《Anno 1602》时，偶然发现了 Sunflower Interactive（《Anno 1602》背后的工作室）制作的这款冷门主机风动作冒险游戏。我发现自己莫名地被其吸引，尤其是被那从来不知道“讽刺”为何物的剧本所打动。
* **数字文化：** 在文化战线上，如果在时间上能安排得开，我正打算写一篇关于 Napster 及其引发的行业颠覆的文章。

> * **Maxis & *The Sims*:** We’re up to our eyebrows in this already, so I assume that no further explanation is required.
> * **Driving Sims:** A hugely neglected topic around these parts. I can’t remedy that entirely, but I can cover how these lifestyle-adjacent games came to be, alongside the one driving game I ever got seriously into (Hint: it involves Porsches).
> * **The *Lemmings* Franchise:** The somewhat anticlimactic later days of the series, which at least concluded with one last really good game.
> * **Major RPGs:** *Deus Ex*, *Diablo II*, *Icewind Dale*, and *Baldur’s Gate II*.
> * **Russian CRPGs:** The Russians are coming! Most notably with *Evil Islands*, an idiosyncratic but engaging take on the CRPG.
> * **City-Builders:** Impressions Games and their historical city-builders.
> * **Her Interactive:** I’ve been promising something about the folks who found fame and fortune—on a modest scale—through the unlikely vehicle of interactive *Nancy Drew* for years now. I’m finally going to do it this year. No, really, I am!
> * **The Adventure Genre:** The ongoing struggle for relevance: *Discworld Noir*; *In Cold Blood*; *Stupid Invaders*; *Escape from Monkey Island*; *RealMyst*; and *Fairy Tale About Father Frost, Ivan, and Nastya* (try saying that last one three times fast...). Not an overly heartening list, but needs must.
> * ***The Longest Journey*:** Given its critical acclaim, this adventure game definitely deserves its own standalone bullet point.
> * ***No One Lives Forever*:** A groovy, desperately needed breath of lightness and whimsy for the gamer-dark, testosterone-overloaded FPS genre (even if it does totally rip off the *Austin Powers* aesthetic).
> * **European Strategy (*Aufbaustrategie*):** The uncanny popularity in Germany and Europe of titles like the *Settlers* series and especially *Anno 1602*, the best-selling German game of all time at the turn of the millennium.
> * ***Technomage: Return of Eternity*:** I stumbled across this obscure curiosity of a console-style action-adventure from Sunflower Interactive (the studio behind *Anno 1602*) while researching the latter. I found myself weirdly charmed, not least by a script that has never heard of the concept of irony.
> * **Digital Culture:** On the cultural front, I’m toying with the idea of an article on Napster and the disruption it caused, if I can squeeze it in.

## 对过往计划的反思与遗珠说明

> ## Reflections on Past Plans and Notable Omissions

回顾我上一份清单，难得的是我这次相当好地兑现了我的承诺。一个例外是《Discworld Noir》，它被顺延到了上面提到的通用冒险游戏文章中。另一个是《The Longest Journey》，它直到 2000 年或更晚才走出挪威本土市场，因此放在这里正合适。

> Looking back over my last list, I managed to do a reasonably good job of keeping my promises for a change. One exception is *Discworld Noir*, which gets pushed into the general adventure article above. Another is *The Longest Journey*, which didn’t reach markets beyond Norway until 2000 or later, so it fits fine here instead. 

至于《Metal Gear Solid》（合金装备）……朋友们，我尽力了。我真的尽力了。但我觉得完全没办法以任何严肃的态度来看待它。如果我写它，我只会把所有时间都花在嘲笑它上面，这只会让你们当中喜欢这种日本主机美学的人更加讨厌我。对不起了，固蛇（Solid Snake）的粉丝们。这不是你们的问题，是我的问题。

> As far as *Metal Gear Solid* goes… I tried, folks. I really did. But I found it impossible to take seriously in any sort of way. If I wrote about it, I’d just spend all my time making fun of it, and that would just make those of you who enjoy this Japanese console aesthetic hate me even more. I’m sorry, Solid Snake fans. It’s not you, it’s me.

像往常一样，非常欢迎大家为 2000 年提最后的建议，或者为未来的选题投稿。我不能保证对所有建议都予以采纳，但我会认真参考，前提是不超出日程排期、个人兴趣、专业知识以及我能把它们写得多么有趣。

> As usual, last-minute suggestions for year 2000 and pitches for future topics are welcome. I can’t promise to act on all of them, but I will take them under advisement, bounded only by scheduling, personal interest, expertise, and how non-boring I can make them.
