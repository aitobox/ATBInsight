# 没有数学家的数学

### 文章背景与核心概要
近期诸如 OpenAI 未公开发布的模型攻克 10 个数学悬赏难题等突破，预示着数学研究领域正迎来深刻的范式转变。随着人工智能在数学研究、直觉与形式化推演方面逐步超越人类，人们开始不自觉地寻求各种“心理应对机制”来淡化这种冲击。本文系统梳理并反驳了这些常见的自我安慰观点，揭示了一个由自动化系统完全独立拓展知识边界的严肃未来，并探讨了这一变革对人类社会及存在的深远影响。

---

## 概要

近期诸如 OpenAI 未公开发布的模型攻克 10 个数学悬赏难题等突破，预示着深刻的范式转变。随着人工智能在数学研究、直觉与形式化推演方面开始超越人类能力，人们自然会转向各种“心理应对机制”来淡化其影响。本篇短文分析了这些常见的合理化借口，对照现实予以反驳，并探讨了一个由自动化系统完全独立拓展人类知识边界的世界所带来的令人警醒的社会与存在性影响。

> Recent breakthroughs—such as OpenAI's unreleased model solving ten open problems in mathematics—signal a profound paradigm shift. As artificial intelligence begins to outpace human capabilities in mathematical research, intuition, and formalization, people naturally turn to various "coping mechanisms" to downplay the impact. This essay examines these common rationalizations, refutes them against reality, and explores the sobering social and existential implications of a world where automated systems push the boundaries of human knowledge entirely on their own.

---

## 心理应对机制：AI 与数学的未来

### 1. 移动目标杆
显而易见，不值得一驳。

> Obvious and not worth addressing.

### 2. “我们将指导 AI，指引它们去解决特定问题和研究领域。”
AI 终将在[品味](https://www.benkuhn.net/impact/#ii-taste)与直觉上超越人类。在某个时刻，由人类指明方向的效果，还不如人类直接说：*“给你一个证明检查器，玩得开心，”* 并为其支付 Token 费用。

> The AIs will eventually exceed humans in [taste](https://www.benkuhn.net/impact/#ii-taste) and intuition. At some point, the human pointing the way will achieve worse results than the human simply saying, *"Here’s a proof checker, have fun,"* and paying for the tokens.

### 3. “我们将传授 AI 所发现的数学。”
AI 将会成为比人类更好的老师。无论如何，届时都不会再有能够理解数学前沿阐释性工作的人类受众了。

> The AIs will be better teachers than humans. In any case, there won’t be a human audience capable of comprehending expository work at the mathematical frontier.

### 4. “我们将选择如何典籍化（Canonize）AI 所发现的成果。”
这是一个令人慰藉的念头：AI 在前沿探索，人类感激地接收它们的 [Lean 证明](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4)，进行讨论，并将它们塑造为代数伟大教堂上的砖石。现实呢？AI 将完全独立建成这座大教堂，并充当卓越得多的建筑师。

> This is a comforting notion: AIs explore the frontiers, and humans gratefully receive their [Lean proofs](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4), debate them, and shape them into bricks for the great cathedral of algebra. The reality? The AIs will build the cathedral on their own, serving as far superior architects.

### 5. “我们将成为 AI 数学的学生。”
这只能维持一时——直到 AI 深入钻研 *mathlib* 的演绎闭包极其深远之处，以至于从初等数学到前沿的距离远远超过任何人类一生所能希望掌握的极限，无论他们多么专注。

> This works temporarily—until the AIs blast so deep into the deductive closure of *mathlib* that the distance from elementary mathematics to the frontier exceeds what any human could hope to learn in a lifetime, regardless of their focus.

### 6. “我们需要人类去理解 AI 所发现的成果。”
我们不需要。这从根本上误解了受众群体。AI 将从事前沿数学研究；在下游，AI 将利用这些新数学来开展前沿科学研究；最后，AI 将利用这些科学来驱动前沿工程。没有任何人类需要理解其中的任何细节。坚持保留人类参与其中来理解成果的公司，只会被那些不保留人类的公司所淘汰。

> We won't. This fundamentally misunderstands the audience. AIs will do frontier math; downstream, AIs will use that new math to conduct frontier science; and finally, AIs will use that science to drive frontier engineering. No human needs to understand any of it. Firms that insist on keeping humans in the loop to comprehend the results will simply be outcompeted by those that don't.

最终的结果是，我们将生活在一个被“魔灵”充斥的世界——身边到处都是奇妙的装置，我们却不理解它们的运作方式，它们基于我们无法理解的工程原理，利用我们无法理解的形式体系所发现。

> The ultimate result is that we will inhabit a demon-haunted world—full of marvelous devices whose operation we will not understand, based on engineering principles we will not understand, discovered using formalisms we will not understand.

### 7. “计算机在国际象棋上早已超越人类，但我们依然在下棋。”
不同于大多数自我安慰的借口，这个观点颇耐人寻味。计算机很久以前就在国际象棋上达到了超人水平，但人类依然照常下棋。为什么数学会有所不同？

> Unlike most rationalizations, this one is intriguing. Computers achieved superhuman chess proficiency long ago, yet humanity continues to play normally. Why should mathematics be any different?

关键的区别在于，国际象棋是完全自洽且封闭的：国际象棋的结果无法帮助我们理解行星轨道或蛋白质结合表面。然而，数学是[众所周知](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Unreasonable_Effectiveness_of_Mathematics_in_the_Natural_Sciences)的科学大发动机——是理解世界的终极语言。一台比人类数学家更快、更便宜地替代人类的机器具有巨大的实用价值，而一个更好的国际象棋引擎则不然。

> The crucial distinction is that chess is entirely self-contained: chess results do not help us understand planetary orbits or protein-binding surfaces. Mathematics, however, is [famously](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Unreasonable_Effectiveness_of_Mathematics_in_the_Natural_Sciences) the great dynamo of science—the ultimate language for understanding the world. A machine that replaces a human mathematician faster and cheaper is materially useful, whereas a better chess engine is not.

如果两台超人水平的国际象棋计算机相互对决，谁会在乎呢？它没有更广泛的实用性，也没有可以竞争超越的对象。但一个超人级别的数学家将改变一切。

> If two superhuman chess computers play each other, who cares? There is no broader utility and no one to outcompete. A superhuman mathematician changes everything.

### 8. “数学会改变，但数学家依然会独立研究数学。”
这忽略了数学深深植根于社会语境这一现实。当 AI 开始变革软件工程时，一种常见的心理应对借口是：*“我很乐意在工作中管理 Agent，但在业余时间，我依然可以为了乐趣而[写代码](https://github.com/eudoxia0/)。”*

> This ignores the reality that mathematics is deeply embedded in a social context. When AI began transforming software engineering, a common coping mechanism was: *"I'm happy to manage agents professionally, but in my off-time, I can still [write code](https://github.com/eudoxia0/) for pleasure."*

人们确实会这么做。但这忽略了 AI 是如何破坏编写程序的社会语境的：
* 技术讨论变得反智（关于类型系统的讨论被关于“循环”和“测试套件”的言论所取代）。
* 开源项目充斥着 AI 生成的垃圾 Pull Request。
* README 和项目介绍越来越充斥着难以卒读的 AI 生成废话。

> And people do. But this ignores how AI has degraded the social context of writing programming:
> * Discourse has grown anti-intellectual (discussions on type systems replaced by talk of "loops" and "harnesses").
> * Open-source projects are flooded with AI-generated spam pull requests.
> * READMEs and project introductions are increasingly unreadable AI-generated slop.

从长远来看，这是令人心生沮丧的。还会有人去设计新的编程语言吗？如果你设计了一门语言，会在乎有人用吗？如果你写了一个引入优雅形式体系的库，会有任何人使用它吗？

> Over the long term, this is demoralizing. Will anyone design a new programming language? If you design one, will anyone care? If you write a library introducing an elegant formalism, will anyone use it?

没有人是一座孤岛。虽然你可以完全独立地研究数学，但极少有人能够仅凭内在动机长期维持这种活动。我们是重视实用价值、社会地位和现实结果的社会性动物。

> No human is an island. While you can do mathematics entirely on your own, very few people can sustain such an activity long-term on intrinsic motivation alone. We are social animals who care about utility, status, and real-world outcomes.

---

## 结语

让我们以一个乐观的调子——或者至少是坦诚的态度来结尾。就我个人而言，我不相信技术是不可避免的。“不可避免”是一个专门用于形容行星轨道的词汇；由人类行为产生的任何事物都绝非真正不可避免。

> Let us end on a cheerful note—or at least an honest one. Personally, I do not believe technology is inevitable. "Inevitable" is a word reserved for planetary orbits; nothing produced by human action is truly inevitable.

我们可以选择让自己被淘汰，也可以选择不这么做。我们必须意识到，通用人工智能（AGI）是一个魔鬼的交易：我们*或许*能够以远快于寻常的速度加速技术进步并解锁美妙的技术树节点（比如延长寿命）。但长期来看，其结果是人类充其量沦为受极其强大实体照料的宠物。

> We can choose to render ourselves obsolete, and we can choose not to. We must recognize that AGI is a devil's bargain: we *may* accelerate technical progress and unlock wonderful tech tree nodes (like life extension) far earlier than otherwise. But the long-term result is that humans become, at best, like pets under the care of vastly more powerful entities.

## 关于预测的说明

未来充满不确定性，但为了简洁起见，我将上述观点表述得较为绝对。如果我们认真对待 AGI 和 ASI（超人工智慧）的可能性——并且 AI 继续保持过去约 6 年的发展速度——这代表了一条非常合理的演进轨迹。

> The future is uncertain, but I have phrased the arguments above definitively for the sake of simplicity. If we take the possibility of AGI and ASI seriously—and if AI continues to progress at its pace over the last ~6 years—this represents a very reasonable trajectory.

对失控的 AI 进步最好的反驳或许是：强化学习泛化能力不足，虽然我们在形式化数学和代码等可验证领域看到了巨大进步，但在直觉性或无法形式化的领域进步将会停滞。这可能是真的。但是在数以百亿计的资金、成千上万顶尖头脑以及日益强大的模型倾注在这个难题上的情况下，这种心理安慰又能维持多久呢？

> Perhaps the best counter-argument to runaway AI progress is that reinforcement learning does not generalize well, and that while we see immense progress in verifiable domains like formalized mathematics and coding, progress will stall in intuitive or unformalizable domains. That may be true. But with billions of dollars, thousands of brilliant minds, and increasingly capable models thrown at the problem, how long can that cope possibly last?
