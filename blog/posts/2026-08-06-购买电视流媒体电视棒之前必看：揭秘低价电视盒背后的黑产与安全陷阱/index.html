购买电视流媒体电视棒之前必看：揭秘低价电视盒背后的黑产与安全陷阱

文章背景与核心概要

长期以来，安全专家一直对租用用户网络连接的无牌电视流媒体盒发出警告。安全公司 Bitsight 的最新突破性调查揭示，包括 H96 在内的热门山寨电视棒（TV Streaming Sticks）已被秘密武器化，卷入了一场由 AI 驱动的大规模广告欺诈网络。当用户的电视关闭时，这些设备会伪装成知名品牌的手机，在 AI 生成的假冒网站上自动点击广告，为黑灰产团伙每天产生数万美元的非法收益，同时使用户的家庭网络陷入严重的安全风险之中。

概要

安全专家长期以来一直警告那些租用用户互联网连接的通用电视流媒体盒。然而，Bitsight 的一项突破性分析揭示，热门的无牌流媒体电视棒（如 H96）也被秘密武器化，卷入了一场由 AI 驱动的大规模广告欺诈行动。通过伪装成移动设备并利用自动化工作流，这些设备会在电视关闭时点击 AI 生成网站上的欺诈性广告，在给用户网络带来严重安全风险的同时，估计每天能为威胁分子带来超过 5 万美元的收益。

Security experts have long warned about generic TV streaming boxes that rent out user internet connections. However, a groundbreaking analysis by Bitsight reveals that popular off-brand streaming sticks—such as the H96—are also secretly weaponized in a massive, AI-driven ad fraud operation. By spoofing mobile devices and utilizing automated workflows, these devices click on fraudulent ads on AI-generated websites when the television is turned off, generating an estimated $50,000+ daily for threat actors while leaving user networks exposed to severe security risks.

广告欺诈的隐藏机制

安全公司 Bitsight 的威胁研究员 Pedro Falé 在收购了一个此前由 H96 流媒体电视棒用于遥测的过期域名后，揭开了这个复杂的广告欺诈网络。该域名定期从全球数万台设备收集硬件配置文件并安装应用程序。

Security firm Bitsight threat researcher Pedro Falé uncovered the complex ad fraud network after acquiring an expired domain previously used for telemetry by H96 streaming sticks. The domain periodically collected hardware profiles and installed apps from tens of thousands of devices worldwide.

然而，流量并没有显示为电视硬件，而是揭示出几乎所有的流媒体盒都在伪装成三星、Vivo、华为和小米等品牌生产的手机。

Instead of recognizing TV hardware, however, the traffic revealed that nearly all the streaming boxes were masquerading as mobile phones manufactured by brands like Samsung, Vivo, Huawei, and Xiaomi.

“我们注意到有些事情严重不对劲，” Falé 表示。“向这个工厂出厂预装的 Android TV Box 后门上报信息的多个设备居然都是‘手机’。”

“We noticed something was wildly wrong,” Falé said. “Multiple devices reporting to this factory Android TV Box backdoor were ‘phones.’”

罪魁祸首：蜂窝集团（Fengwo Group）

遥测数据指向了与浙江蜂窝物联网科技有限公司（以蜂窝集团/Fengwo Group名义运营）绑定的两个预装应用，该公司是一家成立于 2019 年的中国大陆企业。Bitsight 追溯了该行动在香港和新加坡的多个空壳公司身份。

The telemetry data pointed to two pre-installed apps tied to Zhejiang Fengwo IoT Technology Ltd (operating as Fengwo Group), a mainland China-based entity founded in 2019. Bitsight traced the operation back through various shell identities in Hong Kong and Singapore.

这些应用将 H96 流媒体电视棒作为被挟持的流量来源，访问由蜂窝集团创建的 AI 生成新闻和博客网站（涵盖金融、健康、游戏和美食领域）。关键在于，这些网站仅在由匹配被篡改的移动设备配置文件的受害流媒体棒访问时才展示广告。

The apps use the H96 streaming sticks as captive traffic sources to visit AI-generated news and blog sites (covering finance, health, gaming, and food) created by the Fengwo Group. Crucially, these sites only display ads when visited by a device matching the spoofed mobile profile of the compromised streaming sticks.

AI 数字人与 Blockly 驱动的欺诈

蜂窝集团的公开域名 (fwgcloud[.]com) 声称该公司专注于用于客户服务和情感陪护的“AI 数字人”。然而，Bitsight 发现的内部 Wiki 透露了一个更加实用主义的目的：利用 Google Blockly 视觉化编程语言的自定义实现来构建虚假网站。

The Fengwo Group's public domain (fwgcloud[.]com) claims the company specializes in "AI digital humans" for customer service and companionship. However, internal wikis uncovered by Bitsight reveal a far more utilitarian purpose: building sham websites using a proprietary implementation of Google’s Blockly visual programming language.

  • 低技术门槛操作人员： 使用 Blockly，低技能的操作人员只需拖放代码块即可定义自动化欺诈流程，而无需理解底层的技术细节。
  • 成本效益高： 资深开发者创建的模板使公司能够极大地减少技术开销和运营成本。
  • 拟人化导航： 当流媒体棒被选定执行任务时，它会在后台静默启动浏览器、管理标签页，并使用视觉与推理系统来识别并点击广告，就像真实人类用户操作一样。
  • Low-Skilled Operators: Using Blockly, low-skilled operators can drag and drop code blocks to define automated fraud routines without understanding the underlying technicalities.
  • Cost Efficiency: Templates created by expert developers allow the company to minimize technical overhead and operating costs.
  • Human-Like Navigation: When selected for a task, the streaming stick silently launches a browser, manages tabs, and uses vision-and-reasoning systems to identify and click ads just like a human user would.

电视开机：住宅代理；电视关机：广告欺诈

Bitsight 发现，H96 设备会根据设备的使用状态在两种恶意角色之间切换：

Bitsight discovered that the H96 devices alternate between two malicious roles based on how the device is being used:

  1. 电视开机（HDMI 信号处于激活状态）： 该设备充当住宅代理（Residential Proxy），将其家庭互联网连接出租给匿名的第三方（从数据抓取者到网络犯罪分子不等）。
  2. 电视关机： 该设备切换任务以执行广告欺诈流程，确保繁重的计算处理不会干扰用户看电视时的正常视频播放。
  1. TV On (HDMI Signal Active): The device functions as a residential proxy, renting out the user’s home internet connection to anonymous third parties (ranging from scrapers to cybercriminals).
  2. TV Off: The device switches tasks to execute ad fraud routines, ensuring the heavy processing doesn't disrupt normal video streaming when the user is watching TV.

尽管 FBI 和安全行业领袖屡次发出警告，主要电商平台（如亚马逊、百佳、Newegg 等）仍在继续销售预装修改版 Android 系统的未经验证的廉价流媒体盒。

Despite repeated warnings from agencies like the FBI and security industry leaders, major e-commerce platforms (such as Amazon, Best Buy, and Newegg) continue to sell unverified, budget streaming boxes bundled with modified Android operating systems.

资金追踪与家庭网络安全防护

  • 巨额收益： Bitsight 追踪到全球大约 38,000 台设备定期向原蜂窝域名发送数据，保守估计每日广告欺诈收入接近 5 万美元（这还不包括代理服务的收益）。
  • 不安全生态： 由于这些廉价电视盒缺乏安全认证，它们极易沦为僵尸网络的猎物——例如代理追踪机构 Synthient 记录的 Kimwolf 僵尸网络，该网络已奴役了数百万台缺乏安全防护的物联网设备。
  • Massive Revenues: Bitsight tracked roughly 38,000 global devices phoning home to the old Fengwo domain, estimating a conservative ad fraud revenue stream of nearly $50,000 per day (excluding proxy earnings).
  • Insecure Ecosystems: Because these cheap TV boxes lack authentication, they easily fall victim to botnets—such as the Kimwolf botnet documented by proxy tracker Synthient, which has enslaved millions of insecure IoT devices.

如何保护自己：

  • 选择知名品牌： 仅从信誉良好、经过验证的制造商处购买流媒体硬件设备。
  • 验证 Android TV OS： 消费者可以按照 Google 官方说明确认设备是否运行官方 Android TV OS 以及具备 Play Protect 认证。
  • 查阅威胁数据库： 审核已被标记硬件的实时列表，例如 Synthient 的公开研究仓库，该仓库追踪已知出厂预装恶意软件的物联网设备（包括山寨流媒体棒和数码相框）。

How to Protect Yourself:

  • Stick to Name Brands: Only purchase streaming hardware from reputable, verified manufacturers.
  • Verify Android TV OS: Consumers can confirm if a device runs official Android TV OS and Play Protect certification by following Google’s official instructions.
  • Check Threat Databases: Review running lists of flagged hardware, such as Synthient's public research repository, which tracks IoT devices (including off-brand streaming sticks and digital photo frames) known to ship with pre-installed malicious software.
