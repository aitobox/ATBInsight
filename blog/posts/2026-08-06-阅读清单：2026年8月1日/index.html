文章背景与核心概要

本期每周精选速递聚焦于建筑、基础设施和工业技术领域的前沿动态。内容涵盖三大维度：首先是城市与社会趋势，包括大都市幼儿人口减少现状及配备防狼/防暴喷雾无人机的校园安防争议；其次是制造与地缘政治，探讨中国集中资源攻关的芯片“曼哈顿计划”与国产浸没式 DUV 光刻机量产，以及国际间对关键矿产和硬件的出口管制限制；最后是企业与国防产业动态，审视波音飞机的财务复苏困境、特斯拉的战略重组及美军下一代战术卡车的竞标角逐。

执行摘要

欢迎阅读本周关于建筑、基础设施和工业技术的精选新闻与洞察汇总。

Welcome to this week's curated roundup of news and insights focusing on buildings, infrastructure, and industrial technology.

本期亮点包括： * 城市与社会趋势： 主要大都市地区幼童人口下降，以及如防暴喷雾无人机等创新（且具有争议的）校园安防措施。 * 制造与地缘政治： 中国类似于历史原子弹计划的雄心勃勃的“芯片曼哈顿计划”、国产 DUV 光刻机的重大突破，以及国际上对关键矿产和硬件日益收紧的贸易限制。 * 企业与国防动态： 波音公司持续面临的财务复苏障碍、特斯拉的战略调整，以及传统汽车制造商为向美国陆军供货而展开的高风险竞争。

Highlights from this edition include: * Urban & Social Trends: The declining population of young children in major metropolitan areas, alongside innovative (and controversial) school security measures like pepper-spray drones. * Manufacturing & Geopolitics: China's ambitious "chip Manhattan Project" mirroring historical atomic initiatives, major developments in domestic DUV lithography, and tightening international trade restrictions on critical minerals and hardware. * Corporate & Defense Movements: Boeing’s ongoing financial recovery hurdles, Tesla's strategic shifts, and the high-stakes competition between legacy automakers to supply the U.S. Army.

住房与城市

  • 3D 打印军营： 建筑科技公司 ICON 已完成为布利斯堡（Fort Bliss）打印 10 栋营房建筑的工作，前两栋已正式完工并投入使用。
    在 Army 查看更多信息

    • 3D-Printed Military Barracks: Construction tech company ICON has completed printing 10 barracks buildings for Fort Bliss, with the first two units officially finished and occupied.
      Read more at the Army

  • 应对枪击案的防御无人机： 佛罗里达州、佐治亚州和科罗拉多州的至少九所学校正在引入由奥斯汀 Mithril Defense 公司开发的自动化非致命防御无人机。由教师触发后，这些无人机可在 15 秒内赶到威胁地点，通过闪光灯、警报器、胡椒凝胶或控制碰撞使目标丧失方向感。
    在华盛顿邮报查看更多信息

    • Active-Shooter Defense Drones: At least nine schools across Florida, Georgia, and Colorado are adopting automated, non-lethal defense drones developed by Austin-based Mithril Defense. Triggered by teachers, these drones can reach a threat within 15 seconds to disorient targets via flashing strobes, sirens, pepper gel, or controlled impacts.
      Read more in the Washington Post

  • 城市家庭数量下降： 《华尔街日报》最近的一项分析指出，过去十年中，美国主要城市 18 岁以下儿童人口下降了 6%，超过了全国 1% 的下降幅度。在城市中心，含有 5 岁以下儿童的家庭降幅更是高达 15%。
    在华尔街日报查看更多信息

    • The Decline of Urban Families: A recent Wall Street Journal analysis highlights a 6% drop in the population of children under 18 across major U.S. cities over the past decade—outpacing the national decline of 1%. Families with children under 5 saw an even steeper 15% drop in urban centers.
      Read more in the Wall Street Journal

制造与技术

  • 波音前方的财务之路： 据航空研究机构 Leeham Co. 的数据，自 2019 年以来，波音的商用客机部门累计亏损已超过 460 亿美元，在持续的延误和取消潮中，今年每交付一台飞机平均亏损 280 万美元。
    在金融时报查看更多信息

    • Boeing’s Financial Road Ahead: According to aviation research firm Leeham Co., Boeing’s commercial airliner division has accumulated over $46 billion in losses since 2019, averaging a $2.8 million loss per aircraft delivered this year amidst persistent delays and cancellations.
      Read more in the Financial Times

  • 特斯拉战略重组： 有报道称特斯拉正在评估拆分其中国业务的可能性，这或许能为与 SpaceX 的合并扫清道路。此外，该公司还宣布计划开源已停产的 Model S 和 Model X 的设计方案。
    在华尔街日报查看更多信息 以及 Electrek

    • Tesla Strategic Realignments: Reports indicate Tesla is weighing a spinoff of its Chinese operations to potentially pave the way for a merger with SpaceX. Additionally, the company announced plans to open-source designs for the discontinued Model S and Model X.
      Read more via WSJ and Electrek

  • 中国的“芯片曼哈顿计划”： 借鉴其 20 世纪 60 年代核武器计划的中央委员会体制，中国成功动员了顶尖实验室和企业，以克服国内半导体供应链的瓶颈。与此同时，一家本土企业已开始量产国产浸没式深紫外（DUV）光刻机。
    在华尔街日报查看更多信息 以及 Tom’s Hardware

    • China’s "Chip Manhattan Project": Utilizing a centralized committee structure reminiscent of its 1960s nuclear weapons programs, China has successfully mobilized top labs and companies to overcome domestic semiconductor supply chain bottlenecks. Concurrently, a domestic firm has commenced mass production of immersion deep ultraviolet (DUV) lithography machines.
      Read more via WSJ and Tom’s Hardware

  • 全球贸易限制与出口禁令：

  • 美国参议院正在评估一项法案，拟禁止中国持股超过 15% 的汽车制造商进入美国本土市场（这可能波及梅赛德斯-奔驰），同时提出针对外国制造的机器人和光伏逆变器的全新禁令（Starlink 则获得了路由器豁免）。

  • 作为反制，中国已限制向 14 家欧洲企业出口先进制造所需的关键矿产。
    在 Car and Driver 查看更多信息、路透社、Ars Technica 以及 纽约时报

    • Global Trade Restrictions & Export Bans:
    • The U.S. Senate is weighing a bill to bar automakers with >15% Chinese ownership—potentially including Mercedes-Benz—from the domestic market, alongside new bans targeting foreign-made robots and power inverters (with Starlink securing a router exemption).
    • In retaliation, China has restricted critical mineral exports required for advanced manufacturing to 14 European companies.
      Read more via Car and Driver, Reuters, Ars Technica, and the New York Times

  • 军事汽车合同： 福特和通用汽车都在展开激烈竞争，试图夺得为美国陆军提供下一代战术皮卡的合同。
    在华尔街日报查看更多信息

    • Military Automotive Contracts: Both Ford and General Motors are fiercely competing to secure the contract to supply the U.S. Army with its next-generation tactical pickup truck.
      Read more in the Wall Street Journal
