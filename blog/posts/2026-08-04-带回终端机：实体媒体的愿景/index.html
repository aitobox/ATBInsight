带回终端机：实体媒体的愿景

背景与摘要

随着游戏产业加速向纯数字发行转型，实体媒体收藏家们正在哀叹实体游戏的消失。本文回顾了20世纪90年代英国的一个名为 EDOS（软件电子分发）的终端机系统，该系统允许零售商根据需求当场将游戏刻录到磁盘或磁带上。通过重新审视这项技术，文章探讨了在当今数字与实体并行发展的时代，零售商能否通过类似的按需复制模式来解决库存问题。

Summary

随着游戏产业积极转向纯数字发行，实体媒体爱好者正在哀叹实体收藏的流失。本文探讨了 EDOS（软件电子分发）的历史，这是一个 20 世纪 90 年代位于英国的自助终端系统，它允许零售商根据需求将游戏刻录到光盘或磁带上。通过重新审视这项“如果……会怎样”的技术，我们思考现代零售业是否可以通过采用类似的按需复制模式来解决其库存困境。

As the gaming industry shifts aggressively toward digital-only distribution, physical media enthusiasts are mourning the loss of tangible collections. This article explores the history of EDOS (Electronic Distribution of Software), a 1990s UK-based kiosk system that allowed retailers to burn games onto discs or cassettes on demand. By revisiting this "what-if" technology, we consider whether modern retail could solve its inventory woes by adopting similar on-demand duplication models.

The Problem with Modern Retail

走进一家现代游戏商店通常会感觉像是一种受限的体验。虽然网站提供了跨越几十年游戏历史的庞大作品库，但实体商店的货架仅限于当前一代游戏机的最新发布版本。

Walking into a modern game store often feels like a restricted experience. While websites offer a vast library of titles spanning decades of gaming history, physical store shelves are limited to the latest releases for current-gen consoles.

该行业已经远离了实体媒体，但底层技术——CD、DVD 和蓝光光盘——仍然是标准化的。这提出了一个引人注目的问题：为什么零售商不能简单地按需刻录游戏？ 这种系统在理论上可以支持从现代 PlayStation 和 Xbox 游戏到世嘉土星或 Dreamcast 等被遗忘已久的经典游戏的一切。

The industry has moved away from physical media, but the underlying technology—CDs, DVDs, and Blu-rays—remains standardized. This raises a compelling question: Why can’t retailers simply burn games on demand? Such a system could theoretically support everything from modern PlayStation and Xbox titles to long-forgotten classics for the Sega Saturn or Dreamcast.

The EDOS Precedent

在 20 世纪 90 年代初期，英国面临着类似的困境。像 Amstrad CPC、Commodore 64 和 ZX Spectrum 这样的家用电脑很受欢迎，但为特定、不太常见的格式寻找软件却很困难。

In the early 1990s, the UK faced a similar dilemma. Home computers like the Amstrad CPC, Commodore 64, and ZX Spectrum were popular, but finding software for specific, less-common formats was difficult.

Software on Demand Ltd 及其 EDOS 系统 应运而生。这些安装在 John Menzies 等零售商中的庞大机器，是那个时代的奇迹：

Enter Software on Demand Ltd and their EDOS system. These hulking machines, installed in retailers like John Menzies, were marvels of their time: * 多功能性： 配备了软盘驱动器、CD-ROM 驱动器和高速磁带录音机。 * Versatility: Equipped with floppy drives, CD-ROM drives, and high-speed cassette recorders. * 跨平台： 能够为各种家用电脑和早期游戏机复制软件。 * Cross-Platform: Capable of duplicating software for various home computers and early consoles. * 高效： 该系统自动验证每份副本，显著降低了缺陷退货率。 * Efficiency: The system verified each copy automatically, significantly reducing the rate of defective returns.

尽管该公司最终在 1994 年关闭，但 EDOS 系统证明了消费者愿意接受“销售点”制造。这是一个具有前瞻性的解决方案，或许只是超前了几年。

Though the company eventually shuttered in 1994, the EDOS system proved that consumers were willing to embrace "point-of-sale" manufacturing. It was a forward-thinking solution that was perhaps just a few years ahead of its time.

Could It Work Today?

虽然旧游戏的版权管理仍然是一个重大障碍，但现代版 EDOS 的技术基础设施比以往任何时候都更加可行。借助大容量蓝光光盘和高速数字发行，现代自助终端可以轻松处理当今游戏的庞大文件尺寸。

While the rights management for older titles remains a significant hurdle, the technical infrastructure for a modern version of EDOS is more feasible than ever. With high-capacity Blu-ray discs and high-speed digital distribution, a modern kiosk could easily handle the massive file sizes of today’s games.

也许实体媒体的未来不是货架上预先包装好的盒子，而是重返终端机——这是一种在拥抱数字发行便利性的同时，保留拥有实体副本乐趣的方式。

Perhaps the future of physical media isn't pre-packaged boxes on a shelf, but a return to the kiosk—a way to preserve the joy of owning a physical copy while embracing the convenience of digital distribution.
