# Benedict Evans on the New ‘Super App’ ChatGPT

> **Summary:** Industry analyst Benedict Evans and other tech commentators critique OpenAI's newly redesigned desktop application. Riddled with confusing UI choices, forced integrations, and organizational disjointedness, the new "super app" is described as a superficial, sloppy mess that reflects internal corporate chaos rather than thoughtful product design.

---

## Initial Impressions: A UX Disconnect

Analyst Benedict Evans recently took to Threads to share a succinct and scathing review of the redesigned ChatGPT desktop application:

> "Wow, what a total mess.
> 
> What is the difference between a project, a task and a chat?
> 
> Why did chats get a crappy floating window but tasks and projects don’t?
> 
> Why does choosing ‘plugins’ get me ‘templates’?
> 
> Am I not allowed to finish ‘setup’ if I don’t use Slack or Google Drive?"

Navigating the onboarding process proves unnecessarily frustrating—forcing users through setup prompts for third-party integrations like Slack and Google Drive just to clear the interface.

---

## Org Charts Manifested as Software

A classic observation in the tech industry is that companies dominated by internal politics ultimately ship products that mirror their internal organizational chart. 

Given OpenAI's well-documented internal turbulence and high-profile leadership departures regarding safety and structure, the new ChatGPT app serves as a textbook example. At a glance, the app wears the veneer of a polished product, but closer inspection reveals an unstructured, incoherently labeled interface. 

---

## Form Over Function: The "Slop" Aesthetic

While the legacy ChatGPT application remained famously focused—retaining its clean utility on mobile devices and surviving as "ChatGPT Classic" for many desktop users—the new iteration attempts to play dress-up as a heavy-duty productivity suite. 

Critics point to the root cause: an executive obsession driven by FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out) regarding competitors like Anthropic, coupled with a culture where AI researchers hold absolute sway over product designers and app craftspeople. The resulting interface lacks foundational design taste, prompting the fitting comparison to [Homer Simpson's disastrous "Homer" car design](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WPc-VEqBPHI).
