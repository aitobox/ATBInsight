# LLM Cliché Highlighter

> **Summary:** Tired of reading articles crammed with predictable AI-generated phrasing, developer Simon Willison built the **LLM Cliché Highlighter**—a web tool designed to scan text and flag ten of the most common linguistic patterns found in modern LLM writing.

---

## About the Tool

* **Tool Link:** [LLM Cliché Highlighter](https://tools.simonwillison.net/llm-cliche-highlighter)

Frustrated by reading *yet another* article packed with generic filler—the classic "no fluff, no filler, no jargon" style of writing—Simon utilized Fable 5 vibe coding to spin up this lightweight text-analysis utility. 

The application scans pasted text or fetched URLs to highlight ten common repetitive patterns frequently produced by generative AI models.
