# Oxide and Friends: The Open Weight Revolution with Simon Willison

## 📋 Summary
In this episode of the *Oxide and Friends* podcast, Bryan Cantrill and Adam Leventhal are joined by Simon Willison to discuss a whirlwind week in AI. Key topics include the rise of open-weight models (like Kimi K3) rivalling proprietary frontier models, industry-wide open weight lobbying, accidental cybersecurity incidents, and a fascinating array of tangents ranging from historical bioweapons research to aggressive wild turkeys in Alameda. 

---

## 🎙️ Episode Overview

On Monday, I joined Bryan Cantrill and Adam Leventhal on their podcast to discuss a truly *wild* week in tech. 

### Key Discussion Points:
* **The Open Weight Revolution:** Exploring how models like **Kimi K3** prove open-weight alternatives can stand toe-to-toe with proprietary frontier models.
* **AI Policy & Leadership:** The [public letters on Open Weights and American AI Leadership](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/corporate-responsibility/topics/open-weight/) signed by nearly every major AI player—with one [notable exception](https://www.anthropic.com/news/position-open-weights-models).
* **Cybersecurity Incidents:** Discussing [accidental cybersecurity attacks](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/22/openai-cyberattack/). *(Note: The tech moves fast! If we had recorded just a few days later, we also would have covered DeepSeek V4 Flash 0731 and [Anthropic's own embarrassing cyber incident](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/30/three-real-world-incidents/).)*

### Notable Digressions & Tangents
True to the *Oxide and Friends* format, the conversation wandered down several fascinating rabbit holes, including:
* [Golden Gate Claude](https://www.anthropic.com/news/golden-gate-claude)
* The [Zizians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zizians)
