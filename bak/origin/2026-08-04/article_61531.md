# Roblox Set to Introduce AI Game-Building Feature, Including on iOS

## Summary
Roblox is launching a new mobile-first creation tab called "Build" and a suite of AI-powered tools within Studio, enabling its 132 million daily active users to build games using AI agents directly on mobile devices like iOS. This move highlights a glaring double standard regarding Apple's App Store policies, as Apple previously banned independent AI coding apps on iOS while permitting the massive gaming platform to offer similar functionality.

---

## The Announcement
Roblox executives Nick Tornow and Vlad Loktev shared the company's vision on the [Roblox official blog](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2026/07/17/roblox-game-building-ai):

> Twenty years ago, Roblox launched with a simple idea: “You make the game.” At the time, that was a radical proposition — most games were made by studios and professionals, and the idea that anyone could be a creator was far from obvious. But we believed it. And the millions of creators who built on Roblox proved it. As we look to a future where any one of Roblox’s 132 million daily active users could come up with the next hit game, we’re taking that original idea further than we ever have. Over the coming months, we’ll share a series of announcements that will give every creator a clear runway to go further and fulfill the promise of “You make the game” for more people than ever before.
> 
> Today, we’re announcing **Build**, a new mobile-first creation tab within the Roblox app, and a new suite of AI-powered tools within Studio for creators of every level. On July 28, we’ll begin testing these new agentic tools. With Build and Studio, creators can delegate the parts of development that don’t require their full attention.

## The App Store Double Standard

While Roblox's new capability sounds genuinely exciting, it raises significant questions about platform fairness. 

Previously, mobile AI coding apps that ran directly on iOS—such as [Bitrig](https://bitrig.com/), [Replit](https://replit.com/), and [Vibecode](https://www.vibecodeapp.com/)—were [shut down or rejected by Apple](https://mjtsai.com/blog/2026/03/20/updates-to-vibe-coding-apps-rejected-from-the-app-store/) during the winter. While developers remain free to build iOS apps on desktop computers using whatever tools they choose, Apple effectively outlawed the practice of *building iOS apps on iOS*.

Yet, Roblox is seemingly given a pass to introduce AI-assisted game generation directly on mobile platforms. One is left to wonder: Is this policy exception granted simply because Roblox is already an established tech giant with massive user adoption and revenue? How can Apple justify permitting Roblox to deploy this technology while strictly disallowing independent developers from doing the same?
