# An Opinionated Guide to Which AI to Use to Do Stuff

## 📋 Summary
This guide explores the rapid evolution of AI usage, moving away from simple conversational interfaces toward powerful **agentic systems** capable of automating hours of human work. Based on insights from Ethan Mollick’s evolving framework—and commentary on the confusing terminology used by major AI providers—it highlights the shift toward giving AIs access to virtual computers and local environments.

---

## 🔄 The Evolution of AI: From Chat to Agents
A year ago, utilizing AI was primarily about conversation: interacting via chat interfaces like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini using models such as o3, Claude 4 Opus, and Gemini 2.5 Pro, with Deep Research serving as a specialized alternative mode. 

Today, the paradigm has shifted significantly toward **agentic systems**—environments where the AI can autonomously execute tasks equivalent to many hours of real human work in a single run.

Notably, Google’s Gemini has fallen behind in this space due to the lack of an established entry in the Codex/ChatGPT Work/Cowork category, while tools like *Gemini Spark* have yet to prove their utility.

---

## 💻 Giving AI a Computer to Use
Ethan Mollick outlines the mechanics of letting AI interact with computer environments, though he notes the terminology remains notoriously confusing:

> "To use the computers provided by the AI companies, the mode you want is called **ChatGPT Work** in ChatGPT, and **Cowork** in Claude (the naming will not get less confusing, I am sorry to say). [...]
> 
> The most powerful way to use AI is to give it access to your computer. You do that by downloading the ChatGPT or Claude apps and picking a mode to use. ChatGPT's two agent modes are **Work** and **Codex**; Claude's are **Cowork** and **Code**. The names do not map onto each other in any way that will help you remember them. And yes, these use the same names as the Work and Cowork modes we discussed above, but operate differently, and have more features and capabilities because they can access your computer."

---

## ⚙️ Understanding the Unintuitive Interfaces
The distinction between different versions of these features can be remarkably unintuitive. For example, the difference between **ChatGPT Work** on a mobile device versus inside the desktop app (where it acts as a streamlined interface on top of Codex) is striking. 

**Quick Takeaway:** If you switch the ChatGPT mobile app from "Chat" to "Work" mode, you unlock a version where the Code Interpreter container is granted unrestricted access to the internet.
