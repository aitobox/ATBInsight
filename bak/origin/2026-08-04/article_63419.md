# Moonshot AI: Kimi-K3 Release

## 📝 Summary
Moonshot AI has released the model weights for **Kimi-K3**, a massive 2.8 trillion parameter model totaling 1.56TB on Hugging Face. Building upon the licensing strategy introduced with K2, the new K3 license includes stricter conditions for large commercial entities and restricts "Model as a Service" (MaaS) providers making over $20 million annually without a separate agreement. True to their transparency, Moonshot avoids the "open source" label, correctly designating K3 as an **open-weight** model. The model is already accessible via OpenRouter through multiple providers.

---

## 🚀 Release Details
* **Model:** [moonshotai/Kimi-K3](https://huggingface.co/moonshotai/Kimi-K3)
* **Scale:** 2.8 trillion parameters
* **File Size:** 1.56 TB (available on Hugging Face)
* **Availability:** Currently offered via [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/moonshotai/kimi-k3) from 7 providers at standard pricing ($3/million input, $15/million output).

---

## 📜 Licensing Evolution
Moonshot continues to use customized terms for its model releases, explicitly categorizing them as **open-weight** rather than open-source.

### 1. The K2 Precedent (July 2025)
The modified MIT license introduced with Kimi-K2 required prominent UI attribution ("Kimi K2") for commercial products exceeding:
* 100 million monthly active users, OR
* $20 million in monthly revenue.

### 2. The K3 License Updates
The [K3 license](https://huggingface.co/moonshotai/Kimi-K3/blob/main/LICENSE) drops the "modified MIT" branding and introduces tighter restrictions for large-scale commercial services:
> *"If the Licensee or any of its affiliates operates a Model as a Service business, and the aggregate revenue of the Licensee and its affiliates exceeds 20 million US dollars (or the equivalent in other currencies) in total over any consecutive 12 months, the Licensee must enter into a separate agreement with Moonshot AI before using the Software or its derivative works for any commercial purpose."*
