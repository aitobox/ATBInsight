# The Console Wars Have Been Lost

> **Summary:** While it previously seemed that Valve was poised to naturally win the current console generation, Microsoft and Sony have seemingly accelerated the process through unprecedented self-sabotage. Between rising hardware costs, anti-consumer decisions regarding physical media and platform exclusivity, and a lack of compelling software, traditional consoles are losing their value proposition—leaving PC and platforms like Steam as the ultimate victors by default.

---

## Introduction

Previously, I opined that [Valve was about to win the console generation](https://xeiaso.net/blog/2025/valve-is-about-to-win-the-console-generation/). However, I couldn't have possibly predicted that both Microsoft and Sony would just self-sabotage so hard that they’re both going to lose entirely.

## Microsoft and the Xbox Decimation

Between Microsoft's [decimation of the Xbox division](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c36yy27rnpeo), [slaughtering off the IdTech team](https://tech4gamers.com/xbox-lays-off-every-coder-id-software/), and [continued increases in Xbox hardware prices](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/06/25/xbox-console-price-update/), there's nothing left to really be excited about regarding Xbox. 

Sure, their most recent presentation showed off a bunch of exclusives, but none of them made me think, *"Wow, I should go get an Xbox to play that."* Hell, few of them made me think, *"Wow, I should go play that"* at all—save for the *Halo* remake coming out next month, and really, I just want to see how much of a trainwreck that is going to be.

To make matters worse, Microsoft is once again doubling down on making their in-house games strict Xbox exclusives. This strategy fails to incentivize me to play them, because I simply refuse to buy into the hardware just to access the software.

## Sony’s Missteps and the Death of Physical Media

Sony isn't faring much better. They have [discontinued porting their games to PC](https://www.polygon.com/sony-ps5-pc-games-sales-ended/) because they aren't hitting the (probably impossible) revenue targets required to offset massive failures like [*Concord*](https://blog.playstation.com/2024/09/03/an-important-update-on-concord/). 

My PS5 has mostly been relegated to gathering dust when it isn't pulling YouTube and Twitch duty in the living room. It will likely be replaced in favor of my Steam Machine whenever that arrives next year. Nothing coming out in terms of PlayStation exclusives feels compelling enough to justify buying it on a console rather than just waiting for it on Steam, where I can run it on my main rig or home theater PC.

Compounding this, Sony has been [raising prices](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/03/27/new-price-changes-for-ps5-ps5-pro-and-playstation-portal-remote-player/) and recently announced that [they're killing physical media next generation](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/07/01/physical-disc-production-ending-in-january-2028-for-new-games-releasing-on-playstation-consoles/). It makes me seriously wonder if I should even bother getting the next generation of PlayStation. If I can't even hand someone a physical game as a gift anymore, why should I bother buying the new console at all?

In fact, my husband and I both struggle to remember why we even bought a PS5 in the first place. Maybe it was just for quiet couch gaming without aggressive fan noise, or so the video streaming experience from our NAS could properly support HDR.

## The Exception: Nintendo and PC Dominance

We do have a Switch 2 at home, but it's used almost exclusively for Nintendo exclusives like *Mario Kart World* and the *Xenoblade* series. If those exclusives were available on Steam, we wouldn't buy them on the Switch 2 either.

Otherwise, virtually everything we play runs through Steam or other PC storefronts. 

## Conclusion

At the end of the day, Valve really is winning by doing absolutely nothing while the rest of the industry shoots itself in the head. 

My only lingering fear: *What happens when Gabe Newell retires, and the MBA cancer fully infects Valve?*
