# Becoming Skilled at Making Documents

## Summary
Most business documents—from presentations to memos—are poorly designed and fail to communicate effectively. Building on his previous work, Anil Dash has created an open-source **Better Documents `skills.md` file** designed to help users prompt AI tools (like Claude) to generate or review high-quality business documents. Utilizing the concept of "agent skills," the tool applies five core tests to ensure better audience consideration, structure, formatting, and naming conventions.

---

## The Problem with Business Documents
The vast majority of the documents people use to do business are really quite poor:
* Presentations that make your eyes glaze over.
* Memos that are inscrutable or unclear.
* Artifacts that reflect how they were created rather than the message they are meant to convey.

This persistent frustration inspired the original essay, [Make Better Documents](https://www.anildash.com/2024/03/10/make-better-documents/), which captured a list of practical suggestions for creating effective documents that work *for* your goals rather than against them.

## Introducing the Better Documents AI Skill
Following the warm response to those initial suggestions, a new tool has been created: the [Better Documents `skills.md` file](https://github.com/anildash/better-documents). 

This resource helps people who use LLM tools (like Claude) prompt their AI assistants to produce better business documents by default. 

### How Agent Skills Work
If you aren't familiar with them, [agent skills](https://agentskills.io/home) are simple text files (written in Markdown—further proof of [how Markdown is taking over the world](https://www.anildash.com/2026/01/09/how-markdown-took-over-the-world/)) that describe new capabilities or processes for LLMs to use during tasks.

The **Better Documents** skill distills broad design principles into **five essential tests**:
1. Target audience consideration
2. Structural integrity
3. Formatting restraint
4. Clear and intuitive naming
5. Generation of compliant new files or review of existing drafts against best practices

## A Versatile Tool for Everyone
While straightforward, this tool has proven effective for routine document improvement. It is particularly useful even for those who prefer not to have an LLM draft documents from scratch, but who appreciate software assistance similar to a spell-checker or grammar-checker.

## Open Source and Collaboration
If you are familiar with agent skills, the installation process is simple and standard. 

* **Free & Open Source:** The tool is completely free with no restrictions. 
* **Contributions Welcome:** Coders can send a pull request on [GitHub](https://github.com/anildash/better-documents), while non-coders are welcome to share feedback, fixes, or suggestions via email or social media.
