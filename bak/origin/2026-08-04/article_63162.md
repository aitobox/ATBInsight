# An Open Letter to David Sacks

## Summary
This piece critically examines statements made by David Sacks (until recently Trump’s Crypto and AI Czar) regarding US AI primacy and minimal regulation, which were posted in response to a report by Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick and the UK AISI/CAISI assessment of the Kimi-k3s model. The author challenges the rhetoric of initiating an AI cold war with China, advocating instead for international cooperation toward AI for the global good.

---

## The Discourse on US AI Primacy and Regulation

David Sacks recently used current geopolitical events to champion his stance on American primacy and minimal artificial intelligence regulation, responding directly to a statement by Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick. 

You can review the official context via the [CAISI Preliminary Assessment on Kimi-k3s Cyber Capabilities](https://www.nist.gov/news-events/news/2026/07/uk-aisi-caisi-preliminary-assessment-kimi-k3s-cyber-capabilities).

---

## Critique of the Sacks Doctrine

While the findings within the CAISI report itself are not in dispute, the strategic conclusions drawn by figures like Sacks raise serious concerns regarding escalation and global stability. 

Rather than doubling down on a competitive posture destined to mimic a high-stakes technological cold war, the United States should seriously consider pivoting. A more sustainable path involves working *with* China to ensure AI is developed for the collective global good, avoiding an outcome that risks ending uncomfortably for all parties with no clear victors.
