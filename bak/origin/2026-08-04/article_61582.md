# Claude Makes Fable 5 Permanent

## Summary
Responding to intense market competition, Anthropic has reversed its original plan to restrict its flagship **Fable 5** model exclusively to API pricing. Instead, Fable 5 is now being permanently integrated into higher-tier subscription plans, averting the anticipated "Fablepocalypse" for power users—though standard subscribers will still need to rely on usage credits.

---

## Official Announcement
According to an update from [@claudeai](https://twitter.com/claudeai/status/2078302415804379218) on Twitter:

> > Beginning July 20, Claude Fable 5 will be included in all Max and Team Premium plans, at 50% of limits.
> > 
> > Pro and Team Standard users will continue to have access to Fable via usage credits, and will receive a one-time $100 credit.

---

## The Pressure of Market Competition
As noted [last week](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/12/bump/), heavy competition from models like [GPT-5.6 Sol](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/9/gpt-5-6/) (and potentially [Kimi 3](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/16/kimi-k3/)) made Anthropic's initial strategy untenable. 

The core question for consumers was simple: **Why pay $100 or $200 a month for a subscription plan that *doesn't* include Anthropic's best model?** 

## Compute Challenges and Capacity
Anthropic's original hesitation to include Fable 5 in subscriptions came down to compute capacity constraints. It remains to be seen whether they will need to scale back ongoing training efforts to redirect more GPUs toward serving inference for the model.

For now, users can breathe a sigh of relief—the anxiety surrounding the impending loss of subscriber access to Fable 5 has officially come to an end.

> **⚠️ Important Clarification:** 
> Users on the standard $20/month plan still **do not** get direct access to Fable 5 through their subscription. The newly updated access only applies to the Max ($100 and $200/month) and Team Premium plans.
