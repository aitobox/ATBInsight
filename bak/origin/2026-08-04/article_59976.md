# Building Intuition About LLM Parameter Counts

### Summary
While the attention mechanism often dominates our conceptual understanding of Large Language Models (LLMs), it is frequently not the primary driver of a model's total parameter count. By examining the architecture of GPT-2, we discover that token embeddings, output heads, and feed-forward networks (FFNs) account for a surprisingly large share of the total parameters—especially in smaller models.

---

### The "Hidden" Weight of Embeddings
When building a GPT-2 implementation from scratch, it is easy to assume that the "intelligence" of the model—the Transformer blocks—would constitute the vast majority of its size. However, even a stripped-down model with no attention or FFNs can be massive.

With a vocabulary size of 50,257 and an embedding dimension of 768, the input and output matrices alone contain over 38 million parameters each. In a standard 163-million parameter GPT-2 model, these two components account for nearly 50% of the total parameter count.

### Attention vs. Feed-Forward Networks
There is a common misconception that the attention mechanism is the most "expensive" part of an LLM in terms of parameter count. In reality:
*   **Attention Layers:** While complex and central to the model's performance, they often contain fewer parameters than the surrounding architecture.
*   **Feed-Forward Networks (FFNs):** In standard GPT-2 architectures, the FFNs typically contain roughly twice as many parameters as the attention layers.

### Key Takeaways
*   **Vocabulary Size Matters:** As modern models move toward vocabularies with hundreds of thousands of tokens, the embedding and output layers can grow to dominate the entire model.
*   **Don't Overestimate Attention:** While attention is the most difficult component to understand conceptually, it is rarely the primary "weight" in the model's parameter budget.
*   **The "Tiny" Model Trap:** It is entirely possible to build a model that is "all shell and no substance"—a tiny architecture where the vast majority of parameters are simply mapping tokens rather than performing complex reasoning.
