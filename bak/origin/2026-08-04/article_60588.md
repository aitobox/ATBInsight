# Remember Musk’s Suit Alleging a Conspiracy Between Apple and OpenAI?

## Summary
Elon Musk’s previous lawsuit alleging a conspiracy between Apple and OpenAI—sparked by grievances over App Store rankings and his "everything app" ambitions—now appears increasingly unfounded. In light of recent legal friction between Apple and OpenAI, alongside organic editorial rankings, it's clear that App Store placements aren't the result of favoritism. As the author notes, Musk's suspicion likely stems from projection, and given the shifting landscape, it might be time for him to drop the suit altogether.

---

## Background: The Lawsuit

Writing for *Ars Technica* back in August 2025, Ashley Belanger reported:

> After a public [outburst over Grok’s App Store rankings](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2025/08/apple-gets-yanked-into-elon-musks-chatbot-war-with-openai/), on Monday, Elon Musk followed through on his threat to sue Apple and OpenAI.
> 
> At first, Musk appeared fixated on ChatGPT consistently topping Apple’s “Must Have” app list—which Grok has never made—claiming Apple seemed to preference OpenAI, an Apple partner, over all chatbot rivals. But Musk’s [filing](https://cdn.arstechnica.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/X-Corp-v-Apple-8-25-25-Complaint.pdf) shows that the X and xAI owner isn’t just trying to push for more Grok downloads on iPhones—he’s concerned that Apple and OpenAI have teamed up to completely dash his [“everything app” dreams](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2024/03/elon-musks-improbable-path-to-making-x-an-everything-app/), which was [the reason he bought Twitter](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2023/03/fast-moving-musk-makes-very-slow-progress-turning-twitter-into-everything-app/).

## App Store Realities vs. Allegations

For what it’s worth, I just looked at the [editorially-curated “Popular iPhone Apps” list](https://daringfireball.net/misc/2026/06/app-store-popular-list-2026-07-13.png), and ChatGPT is in the top spot. Given [the lawsuit Apple filed](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2026/07/10/apple-sues-openai) against OpenAI last week, I’d say this is pretty good anecdata that these editorial decisions in the App Store aren’t driven by favoritism. The top 10 downloads list for free iPhone apps currently looks like this (for me here in the U.S. App Store):

1. Paramount+
2. Netflix Game Controller
3. ChatGPT
4. Kalshi: Trade the World Cup
5. Peacock TV: Stream TV & Movies
6. Netshort - Popular Dramas & TV
7. Threads

10. Depop - Buy & Sell Clothes

There are some real winners on that list. But only one AI app: ChatGPT.

## Projection and Paranoia

Musk alleges that the top downloads list is crooked too. That’s just projection. If Musk ran a popular App Store he’d put his thumb on the scale to make sure his own apps always top the list. That’s [what he’s done with his personal account](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2026/06/05/musk-x-freak-show), and accounts aligned with his politics, on Twitter/X. 

Because that’s what he would do, he thinks that’s what Apple does. I really do think he believes the App Store’s top download lists are fixed, and that Grok is on the wrong side of the fix. Crooks think everyone is crooked. It’s just one of several ways that Musk is not hooked up right.

I suspect, though, that [he’s no longer worried](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2026/07/12/altman-musk) that Apple is putting its thumb on the scale to favor OpenAI. Maybe he should *never-mind* this lawsuit.
