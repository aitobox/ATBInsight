# Reading List: July 25, 2026

### Summary
This week’s roundup explores the intersection of industrial policy, infrastructure, and energy innovation. Key highlights include the evolving landscape of China’s EV subsidies, the regulatory hurdles facing clean energy projects under the National Historic Preservation Act (NHPA), and strategic insights from the geothermal sector on adopting "learning-by-doing" methodologies inspired by SpaceX.

---

### Housing and Cities
*   **Market Outlook:** The CEO of DR Horton, the largest U.S. homebuilder, expresses skepticism regarding the short-term impact of the *21st Century ROAD to Housing Act* on supply and demand.
*   **The Case for Luxury Housing:** The *Financial Times* argues that high-end developments are essential for market health, as they trigger "filtering"—a process where new supply allows higher-income households to move, subsequently freeing up older, more affordable stock.
*   **Corporate Relocation:** Clarification emerges regarding the Saronic/California Forever situation; reports suggest that California was never a serious contender, and the company’s overtures were likely a strategic move to leverage better terms in Texas.
*   **Market Trends:** Data from ResiClub tracks the shifting landscape of U.S. housing markets, while a visual breakdown highlights U.S. cities that have fallen below the 100,000-population threshold.

### Manufacturing
*   **Semiconductor Expansion:** TSMC is accelerating its investment in Arizona fabrication plants to meet surging AI-driven demand. Additionally, the company has announced plans to increase wafer manufacturing prices by up to 10% starting in 2027.
*   **EV Industry Shifts:** As China’s EV sector matures, the government is beginning to roll back subsidies. Notably, a new consumption tax on lithium-ion batteries is set to take effect this September.

### Energy
*   **Regulatory Friction:** Research indicates that the National Historic Preservation Act (NHPA) creates a disproportionate burden for clean energy projects. Wind and solar developments, due to their larger physical footprints, are 10 times more likely to face "adverse effect" findings compared to fossil fuel projects, leading to significant delays.
*   **Geothermal Innovation:** Tim Latimer, CEO of Fervo Energy, discusses the "SpaceX model" for geothermal development. By prioritizing incremental, "learning-by-doing" projects—such as their work with near-field Enhanced Geothermal Systems (NF-EGS)—the industry can mitigate the risks associated with attempting overly ambitious, large-scale technological leaps in a single phase.
