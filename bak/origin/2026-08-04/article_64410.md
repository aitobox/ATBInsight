## Summary

Welcome to this week's weekly roundup of news and links covering buildings, infrastructure, and industrial technology. Highlights for this week include the deployment of pepper-spray drones in schools, China's "chip Manhattan Project," developments in manufacturing and aerospace, and shifting international trade policies regarding critical minerals and tech.

## Housing and Cities

* **3D-Printed Military Housing:** Icon has completed printing 10 barracks buildings for Fort Bliss, with the first two now fully completed and occupied. [[Army]](https://www.army.mil/article/294240)
* **Active-Shooter Defense Drones:** At least nine schools across Florida, Georgia, and Colorado are installing storage boxes for nonlethal, pepper-spray-equipped drones made by Mithril Defense. Designed to reach a shooter within 15 seconds, the drones can flash strobes, sound sirens, deploy pepper gel, or ram targets at 60 mph. [[Washington Post]](https://www.washingtonpost.com/nation/2026/07/28/schools-are-adding-pepper-spraying-drones-help-combat-active-shooters/)
* **Decline of City Children:** A *Wall Street Journal* analysis highlights a 6% drop in children under 18 living in major U.S. cities over the past decade, led by a 15% decline in children under age 5—even in cities experiencing overall population growth. [[WSJ]](https://www.wsj.com/us-news/cities-losing-families-children-a860a9b1)

## Manufacturing & Technology

* **Boeing's Financial Recovery:** Aviation research firm Leeham Co. estimates that Boeing's commercial airliner division has lost over $46 billion since 2019, averaging a $2.8 million loss on each of the 314 planes delivered this year. [[FT]](https://www.ft.com/content/229f215f-9be0-4afe-a4d7-378aadbf87c5?syn-25a6b1a6=1)
* **Tesla Restructuring Speculation:** Tesla is reportedly considering spinning off its China operation into a separate business, potentially clearing the path for a merger with SpaceX. [[WSJ]](https://www.wsj.com/business/autos/tesla-weighs-sale-of-china-business-to-pave-way-for-potential-spacex-merger-5ae26026)
* **China's "Chip Manhattan Project":** China has mobilized an all-out, state-directed effort mirroring its 1960s nuclear programs to master domestic semiconductor supply chains and catch up to U.S. chipmaking technology. [[WSJ]](https://www.wsj.com/world/china/china-ai-chips-race-949050d0)
* **Domestic DUV Production:** A Chinese company has officially begun mass production of domestic immersion deep ultraviolet (DUV) semiconductor lithography machines, the technology preceding EUV. [[Tom’s Hardware]](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/semiconductors/china-begins-mass-production-of-domestic-immersion-duv-lithography-machines)
* **Open-Sourcing Legacy Models:** Tesla announced plans to open-source the designs for the discontinued Model S, Model X, and Roadster. [[Electrek]](https://electrek.co/2026/07/24/tesla-open-source-model-s-x-roadster-release-audit/)
* **Trade Bans and Restrictions:**
  * **Automotive:** The U.S. Senate is drafting a bill to ban automakers with over 15% Chinese ownership—which could include Mercedes-Benz—from selling cars in the U.S. [[Car and Driver]](https://www.caranddriver.com/news/a73234160/mercedes-benz-potential-ban-us-policy/)
  * **Robotics & Power:** The U.S. government banned the import of new models of Chinese robots and power inverters to protect domestic AI infrastructure. [[Reuters]](https://www.reuters.com/world/trump-administration-ban-new-chinese-robots-inverters-protecting-us-ai-buildout-2026-07-28/)
  * **Exceptions:** Starlink has secured an exemption from a recent FCC ban targeting foreign-made routers. [[Ars Technica]](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/07/starlink-gets-exemption-from-fcc-ban-on-routers-made-outside-the-us/)
  * **Critical Minerals:** China blocked the export of military-applicable critical minerals to 14 European companies, threatening supply chains for wind turbines, electric vehicles, and advanced manufacturing. [[NYT]](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/24/business/china-europe-rare-earths.html)
* **Military Procurement:** Ford and GM are going head-to-head to supply the U.S. Army with a new tactical pickup truck. [[WSJ]](https://www.wsj.com/business/autos/ford-motor-joins-race-to-build-next-u-s-army-truck-f8efa051)
