# John Dvorak on *Computer Chronicles* (1987): The IBM PS/2 Review

> **Summary:** A classic 1987 *Computer Chronicles* clip featuring tech commentator John C. Dvorak discussing the newly released IBM PS/2. While Dvorak is often remembered for his famously incorrect tech predictions, his analysis of the PS/2 was remarkably prescient: praising its cable-free engineering while accurately predicting its commercial failure against messy, commodity-built PC competitors.

## 💡 Analysis: Right for a Change

While John Dvorak is best known for [all](https://en.wikiquote.org/wiki/John_C._Dvorak) [the](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2011/12/23/dvorak) [takes](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2025/11/05/dvorak-itime) he was [jacktastically wrong](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2011/08/11/dvorak) about, what gave his schtick true staying power was his underlying technical savvy. 

His critique of the IBM PS/2 in this episode was spot-on:
* **The Good:** He recognized that it was [technically impressive in many ways](https://www.ibm.com/history/ps-2)—most notably its innovative, cable-free internal assembly.
* **The Reality:** He correctly deduced that the system was ultimately doomed in the broader market when competing against the cheaper, uglier, "mess-of-cables" commodity PCs.
