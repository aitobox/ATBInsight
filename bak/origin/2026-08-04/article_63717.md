## Enshittification and Reverse Centaurs go global

It's safe to say that the past couple of years have been good ones publishing-wise, thanks to a string of international bestsellers, both novels (*Picks and Shovels*) and nonfiction (*Enshittification*, *Reverse Centaur*), as well as plenty of awards and accolades. 

Over the past two months, I've won the Locus Award (*Enshittification*), had a *NYT* bestseller (*Reverse Centaur*), gotten a word in the OED ("enshittification"), and had the number one bestselling nonfiction paperback in Canada for more than a month running (*Reverse Centaur*). I also turned 55—and my radiologist told me I'm now cancer-free, so it's been a good summer all around.

These books have done especially well internationally because they deal with technopolitics, which means my readers are disproportionately "Internet People"—folks who are more likely to speak English, even if they live outside the Anglosphere. 

This post is primarily for those readers who often write in, asking whether there is a translation coming in their own language so they can share the books with their less online, less English-conversant friends.
